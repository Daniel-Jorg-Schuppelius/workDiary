Under **Times** you see the working hours your contractor has recorded on
your company's projects. The page is called **Time entries** and is for
viewing only: times are recorded, corrected and billed exclusively by your
contractor.

## What you see

Your contractor decides how detailed the list is for your company. There are
three levels:

- Totals view: a table with the columns **Month**, **Project** and
  **Hours**. For each month and project it shows the total time, with the
  most recent month at the top. Individual entries are not shown at this
  level.
- Individual entries: for each entry the **Date**, **Duration**, **Project**
  and **Employee**, i.e. who carried out the work.
- Individual entries with description: in addition, the **Description**
  column with the activity text. It only appears for entries your contractor
  has explicitly published for you; all other entries show “—” there.

Hours and durations are shown as hours:minutes, for example 1:30 for one and
a half hours. Individual entries are sorted by date with the most recent at
the top. Each page shows 25 entries; use the page numbers at the bottom to
see more.

## Which times appear

- Only times on projects assigned to your company.
- As a rule, only entries your contractor has published for you. The
  contractor can instead release all times of your projects.
- Internal details such as costs, hourly rates, wage values, tags or internal
  comments are not visible at any level.

If an entry you expect is missing, it has not been published yet or is not
assigned to one of your projects.

## Asking a query about an entry

If the **Questions** area is also released for your company, each row of the
individual entries ends with a **Query** button. It opens the **Ask a query**
form for exactly this time entry. Your question goes to your contractor; you
read the answer under **Questions**. The totals view has no such button.

## Limits

- You cannot change, delete or confirm times.
- Your contractor decides whether the area appears and how detailed it is.
  If it is not released, the **Times** menu item is missing.
