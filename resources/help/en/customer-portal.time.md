Under **Time records** you see the working times recorded on your
company's projects. This lets you understand when and to what extent work
was done on your jobs.

The list is sorted by date, with the most recent entry at the top. Each
row shows the **date** with time, the related **project**, the **staff
member** who carried out the work and a short **description** of the
activity.

When there are many entries, use the page navigation at the bottom to
browse the list. Time tracking is provided for your reference and
traceability; the times themselves are recorded and managed by the
company you commissioned.
