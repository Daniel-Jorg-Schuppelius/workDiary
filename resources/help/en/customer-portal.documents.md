Under **Documents** you find the documents your contractor has explicitly
released for your company, such as test reports, certificates, contracts or
manuals. You can download them there at any time.

## The list

The table shows for each document:

- **Title** – the name of the document.
- **Type** – the document type: **Contract**, **Test report**,
  **Certificate**, **Manual**, **Datasheet**, **Manufacturer document**,
  **Permit**, **Insurance**, **Invoice** or **Other**.
- **Valid until** – the expiry date if the document has one (for example a
  certificate or a permit); otherwise it shows “—”.
- **Released on** – the day your contractor released the document for you.

The most recently released documents are at the top. Each page shows 25
documents; use the page numbers at the bottom to see more.

## Downloading a document

Click **Download** in the row. You always receive the current version of the
document with its original file name. If only “—” appears there, no file is
available for the document yet.

## Asking a query about a document

If the **Questions** area is also released for your company, a **Query**
button appears next to **Download**. Use it to ask a question or leave a
comment about exactly this document; your contractor's answer appears under
**Questions**.

## Which documents appear

- Only documents your contractor has explicitly released to the customer
  portal. Internal documents stay hidden.
- Only documents assigned to your company itself or to your projects, jobs
  and objects.
- If your contractor withdraws a release, the document disappears from the
  list and can no longer be downloaded.

## Limits

You cannot upload, change or delete documents in the portal. If a document
you expect is missing, it has not been released for you yet; please contact
your contact person. The area only appears if it is released for your
company.
