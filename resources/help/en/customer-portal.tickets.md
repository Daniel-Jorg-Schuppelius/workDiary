Under **Tickets** you report requests and faults and follow their progress.

- **New ticket**: describe the issue as precisely as possible and attach photos or files if needed.
- **Service catalog**: common services can be requested directly from the catalog.
- **Known errors**: check first whether a solution or workaround already exists for your problem.

The ticket view shows status and replies, and you can reply yourself.
