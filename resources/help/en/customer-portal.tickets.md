Under **Tickets** you report faults and requests to your contractor's service
team and follow their progress. The area includes the **Service catalog**,
where you order standard services. If it is released for your company, the
**Known errors** page is added.

## My tickets

The **My tickets** page lists your company's tickets, including those that
were not created through the portal. The columns:

- **Number** – the ticket number to quote when you get in touch.
- **Title** – a click opens the ticket.
- **Status** – the processing status (see below).
- **Reported** – the date of the report.

The newest reports are at the top, 25 per page.

## Report a new ticket

1. At the top, expand **Report a new ticket**.
2. Enter a **Short description** (at least three characters); it becomes the
   title of the ticket.
3. Under **What happened?** describe the issue as precisely as possible: what
   you did, what happened next and since when.
4. If needed, attach up to five files, such as photos or screenshots of an
   error message.
5. Click **Create ticket**.

The ticket goes to the service team, and the portal immediately opens the
ticket view.

## The ticket view

The top shows the title, ticket number and status. If your contractor has
agreed resolution deadlines with you, **Resolution promised by** also appears
there with date and time.

Below follows the history, newest entry first: replies from the service team,
your own messages, status notices and files. Download files with a single
click. You never see the team's internal notes.

To reply, write in the **Your reply…** field, attach files if needed and
click **Reply** (the button below the field). The responsible agent is
notified. If the ticket is waiting for your feedback (**Waiting for
customer**), your reply automatically resumes processing.

## Confirm resolution or reopen

When a ticket is **Resolved**, you have two options:

- **Confirm resolution** – the issue is settled; the ticket changes to
  **Accepted**.
- **Reopen** – the problem persists. Enter at least five characters under
  **Reason for reopening**. The ticket returns to **In progress**, and your
  reason appears as a message in the history.

## Rate the handling

When a ticket is **Resolved**, **Accepted**, **Closed** or **Rejected**, the
portal asks once: **How satisfied were you with the handling?** Choose a
level from **Very satisfied** to **Very dissatisfied**, add a **Comment
(optional)** if you like and click **Rate**. Only one rating is possible per
ticket.

## The status values

- **Reported** – received, not yet reviewed.
- **Triaged** – reviewed and classified.
- **Scheduled** – the work has been scheduled.
- **In progress** – the team is working on it.
- **Waiting for customer** – the team needs an answer from you.
- **Waiting for third party** – the team is waiting for someone else, such as
  a manufacturer.
- **Paused** – work is temporarily on hold.
- **Resolved** – the team considers the issue settled; please confirm or
  reopen.
- **Accepted** – you have confirmed the resolution.
- **Closed** – the case is finished.
- **Rejected** – the request will not be handled.

## Ordering services from the service catalog

The **Service catalog** lists standard services with name and description.
If it shows **No services can be ordered at the moment.**, your contractor
has not released any service for you.

1. Click **Order** next to the service you want.
2. Fill in the details the service asks for. If none are needed, the page
   shows **No further details are required for this service.** File and
   signature fields are not part of an order.
3. Click **Submit order**.

The order creates a ticket that opens immediately. If the service needs
approval, it is only carried out after approval.

**My orders** lists your orders with **Number** (the ticket number; a click
opens the ticket), **Service**, **Status** and **Ordered on**. The status is
**Awaiting approval** or **Rejected** while approval is pending or has been
refused; after that it shows the status of the ticket.

If the **Requests & orders** area is also released, the order page offers a
**Request service** button. Use it to request the service first, for example
to receive a quote or to send files along; the order is only placed once you
accept.

## Known errors

The **Known errors** page lists faults your contractor already knows about
and is working on a permanent fix for. Where available, each entry includes a
**Workaround**: how to get around the problem until then. Check the list
before reporting a new ticket. The page is read-only.

## Limits

- You only see the tickets of your own company.
- Sent messages and files cannot be changed or deleted.
- The service team sets priority, responsibility and status; you can
  confirm, reopen and rate.
- When an IT request under **Requests & orders** is commissioned, it also
  becomes a ticket that appears here.
