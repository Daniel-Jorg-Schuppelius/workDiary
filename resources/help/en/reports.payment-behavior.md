Behavior and trend view of **locally managed invoices** — the billing
report shows the inventory (status, aging), this report shows the
behavior behind it.

- **DSO** (days sales outstanding): open receivables at month end
  ÷ revenue of the last 90 days × 90. The higher, the longer
  liquidity is tied up.
- **Days to pay**: days from issue to payment — as a monthly trend
  and as a distribution (box plot) per customer.
- **Delay**: days past due; the top list shows customers with the
  highest average delay.
- **Overdue open invoices** as of the period end.

If an external system (DATEV/Lexoffice) manages the invoices, there is
no data basis here — the report states this openly.
