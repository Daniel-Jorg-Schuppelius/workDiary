The notification center gathers all notices addressed to you in one
place. It shows only your own notifications; no special permission is
required for this. The list can be paged through, and the number of
unread entries is shown separately.

You can mark individual notifications as read or mark all of them as
read at once. If a notification points to a target within WorkDiary,
opening it takes you straight there; external references are not
followed for security reasons.

Whether you additionally receive notifications by email, and during
which hours you should stay quiet, is controlled through the
notification settings in your profile. The collection in the
notification center is unaffected by this.

**Text messages (SMS):** For genuinely critical alerts — such as on-call
notifications — a text message can be delivered in addition. This route is
deliberately frugal: there is a monthly quota, long texts are shortened, and
the organisation is warned before the quota runs out. Text messages do not
replace in-app notifications; they accompany them for the case where nobody is
at a screen.
