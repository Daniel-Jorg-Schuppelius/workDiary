Per diem rates store the flat allowances for additional meal expenses
during travel, separated by country and optionally by region. Each rate is
valid from a date and may have an end date, so several periods can coexist.

For each rate, maintain the full-day amount and the partial-day amount; an
optional overnight amount can be added. Country and currency are stored as
country and currency codes, and the source field supports traceability.

The country filter and column sorting help you locate the relevant rates
quickly. When statutory amounts change, create new rates with the
appropriate start date rather than overwriting existing ones.
