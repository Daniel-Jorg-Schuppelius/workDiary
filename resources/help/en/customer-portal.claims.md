Under **Complaints** you follow your company's complaints: how far the review
has progressed and which returns are running for them. You can submit
additional information there. With **Register a return** you announce a
return yourself; a complaint is created from it automatically. Both pages are
released separately and only appear if they are enabled for your company.

## My complaints

The **My complaints** page shows all complaints of your company with
**Number**, **Title**, **Status** and **Reported on**, the newest at the top,
20 per page. A click on the number opens the complaint.

The status values:

- **Received** – your contractor has the complaint.
- **Under assessment** – it is being reviewed.
- **Decided** – a decision on the complaint has been made.
- **In implementation** – the agreed measure is under way.
- **Completed** – the case is settled.
- **Rejected** – the complaint was not accepted.
- **Withdrawn** – the complaint was withdrawn.

## The complaint in detail

The detail page shows the number, title, status, the **Reported on** date
and the description. Under **Returns** you find the related return numbers
with their status:

- **Announced** – the return is registered, the goods have not arrived yet.
- **Goods receipt recorded** – the goods have arrived.
- **Under review** – the goods are being inspected.
- **Completed** – the inspection of the return is finished.

If your contractor has provided a return label, the link **Download return
label** appears next to the return number. It is only shown if **Register a
return** is also released for your company.

Internal assessments, decision details and amounts of the complaint are not
visible in the portal.

## Submitting additional information

In the **Subsequent submission** section you add information to your
complaint: write in the **Your message …** field (3 to 2000 characters) and
click **Submit**. The message is stored as evidence on the complaint, and the
portal confirms **Subsequent submission transmitted.** Below it, your previous
submissions are listed under **Your subsequent submissions** with their time,
the newest at the top. The responsible person at your contractor is notified
of every submission. Files or photos cannot be attached here.

## Register a return

With **Register a return** you announce a return. You can choose from your
company's delivered shipments and objects. If there are none, the page shows
**There are no deliveries or objects for your account.**

1. Under **Delivery** select the affected delivery (item, item number and
   delivery date) or under **Object** the affected device. One of the two is
   required.
2. If the delivery has serial numbers, enter the number of the affected
   piece under **Serial number**; the field suggests the delivered numbers.
   A number that does not belong to the delivery is rejected.
3. Enter the **Quantity** (prefilled with 1).
4. Enter a **Short description** and explain under **Reason for return** (at
   least ten characters) what is wrong.
5. If needed, add images or PDF files under **Photos or documents (max. 5)**.
6. Click **Register return**.

The portal then shows the new complaint number and the return number. If
**Complaints** is released, the new complaint opens directly; otherwise you
stay on the **Register a return** page. There, **My returns** lists all
returns of your company with **Return number**, **Complaint** and **Status**. As
soon as your contractor provides a return label, **Download return label**
appears next to it – even without the **Complaints** release. Do not send
goods back until the return is registered.

## Limits

- You cannot create a complaint without a return in the portal; please
  contact your contact person for that.
- Your contractor maintains the status, decision and returns.
- You cannot change or withdraw a registered return in the portal.
