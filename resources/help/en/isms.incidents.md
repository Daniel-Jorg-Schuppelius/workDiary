In the **security incidents** register you record, assess and document
information security incidents – regardless of whether personal data is
involved.

Typical workflow:

1. **Record the incident**: title, category (for example "Malware",
   "Phishing", "Unauthorized access", "Service outage"), severity
   ("Low" to "Critical"), the detection/occurrence time and the owner.
2. **Contain and investigate**: capture impact and communication, and
   move the incident along the status chain – from "Reported" through
   "Triage", "Contained", "Eradicated" and "Recovered" to "Closed".
3. **Close**: closing requires a **root-cause analysis** and **lessons
   learned** – without these an incident cannot be closed.
4. **Feed back**: link the incident to affected risks and measures so
   that findings flow back into the risk and control assessment.

Privacy: if personal data is involved, tick the corresponding box. This
is a **pointer** – the notifiable privacy report runs separately in the
data-protection management. The case files are deliberately **not
merged**; you may optionally store a reference to the related privacy
incident.

Permissions: viewing requires ISMS read rights, creating and editing
require ISMS maintenance rights.

Next steps: newly reported **critical** incidents trigger a notification
to management.
