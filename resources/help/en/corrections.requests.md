Correction requests let you add forgotten or incorrect punches without
hiding existing bookings through compensating entries. A request bundles, for
a given date, a reason and individual items that each create, change or
delete a time booking or attendance period. A new request starts as a draft.

The overview shows your own requests as well as those submitted on your
behalf, with their status. With the appropriate permission, HR and team
leads can submit corrections on behalf of other employees; the affected
person remains the owner while you are the requester.

Submitted requests either go through approval or are applied directly as a
self-correction, depending on the organization's configuration. Applied
corrections are always marked as manual entries so that the change and its
reason remain traceable. Drafts and submitted requests can be withdrawn.
