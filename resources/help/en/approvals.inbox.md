The approval inbox collects every approval waiting for you: service
requests, changes and contract negotiations. It is part of the core and
available to every organisation; which items appear depends on the
enabled modules.

**What appears:** Per item only the next open stage, and only if you are
responsible: as the named person, through your role or, for contract
negotiations, through the role your organisation assigns to the stage
type (default: commercial → accounting, technical → team lead,
HR → personnel administration). Only the current round counts.

**Deciding:** Approve, reject, query and delegate work as on the record.
A rejection ends the round; you cannot approve your own request.

**Permission:** The inbox is visible to anyone with the permission to
approve requests; administrators always see it.
