The approval inbox collects every approval waiting for you: service
requests, changes, contract negotiations and investment requests. It is
part of the core and available to every organisation; which items appear
depends on the enabled modules.

**What appears:** Per item only the next open stage, and only if you are
responsible: as the named person, through your role or, for contract
negotiations and investment requests, through the role your organisation
assigns to the stage type (default: commercial → accounting, technical →
team lead, HR → personnel administration, management → management).
Only the current round counts.

**Notification:** When an investment request is submitted or its next
stage is due, the people responsible for that stage are notified — the
requester is not.

**Deciding:** Approve, reject, query and delegate work as on the record.
A rejection ends the round; you cannot approve your own request, and
whoever decided one stage does not also decide the next. Investment
requests can only be decided by people allowed to approve investments;
you can only delegate to such people.

**Permission:** The inbox is visible to anyone with the permission to
approve requests; administrators always see it.
