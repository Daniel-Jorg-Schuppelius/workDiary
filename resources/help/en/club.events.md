Club events are calendar events with club details: training or rehearsal,
course, exam, meeting or another event. There is no second calendar — room,
leader and series work like for any other event.

**Target groups and visibility:** An event addresses the whole club, specific
groups or personally invited members. The target list shows the members of
the target groups on the event day — each member once, even if they belong to
several target groups. Visible does not mean admitted: only members of the
target groups or invited members may register.

**Registration and waiting list:** Capacity counts user and member
participations together. If the event is full, a registration goes to the
waiting list; when a seat frees up, the longest waiting person moves up
automatically. Two simultaneous registrations for the last seat result in
exactly one confirmation. Registration and cancellation deadlines are stored
as hours before the start; administration and group leaders may also add
members spontaneously — capacity still applies.

**Series:** A recurrence creates separate events with their own participant
and attendance lists. Changes and cancellations apply either to the single
event or from this event to all following ones. Rescheduling or cancelling
never deletes registrations — they are kept as history.

**Who may do what:** The register sees all club events; group leaders see and
operate only events of their own target groups. Creating, changing and
cancelling events remains with the club administration.
