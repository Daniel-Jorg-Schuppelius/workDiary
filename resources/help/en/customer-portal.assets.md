Under **Objects** you see the devices and installations that are managed for
your company – with name and serial number. Clicking the name opens the object
file.

The object file shows:

- **Inspection & maintenance appointments** – the stored plans with the next
  due date.
- **Completed maintenance** – what was done and when.
- **Protocols** – the protocols for the object that have been released to
  you.

The view is read-only. Internal notes – such as fault details or assignments –
do not appear here. If an object is missing or a detail is wrong, contact your
contact person.

The area is only visible if it has been enabled for your company.
