Under **Objects** you see the devices and installations your contractor
manages for your company: with their inspection and maintenance dates, the
completed maintenance and the signed protocols released for you. The view
is read-only; you download protocols as PDF.

## The object list

The list shows your company's objects in alphabetical order with **Name** and
**Serial number**, 25 per page. A click on the name opens the object record.
If it shows **No objects available.**, your contractor does not yet manage
any objects for your company.

## The object record

The top shows the name and serial number of the object, followed by three
sections:

- **Inspection & maintenance appointments** – the running inspection and
  maintenance plans with **Name** and **Next due date**, the next due date
  first. **Contractual obligation** marks an appointment that your contract
  requires. Paused plans are not shown because no appointment is pending for
  them. Without a running plan it shows **No appointments configured.**
- **Completed maintenance** – completed maintenance work with name and date.
- **Protocols** – the signed protocols for the object that your contractor
  has released for you, with title, status and date, the newest first, each
  with **Download PDF**. Without such protocols it reads
  **No approved protocols.**

## Accepting protocols

If a protocol is waiting for your acceptance, the section **Awaiting acceptance** appears above the **Protocols** list. **View and sign** opens the protocol with all its items. To accept it, enter your name, sign in the signature box with your finger, a pen or the mouse, confirm that the details are correct and click **Sign**. The signature cannot be withdrawn.

If you have questions or objections, clarify them with your contractor before signing – via the queries, if they are released to you. After your signature your contractor completes the protocol; it is then available for download under **Protocols**.

If you do not agree with the protocol, use **Reject** below it: state a reason and, if needed, name individual defects. Your contractor is notified and revises the protocol; it appears again under **Awaiting acceptance** as soon as it is resubmitted.

## Downloading protocols

The portal only shows signed protocols; drafts and protocols under review
appear once they are signed. The status reads **Signed**, for older protocols
also **Archived** or **Superseded**. With **Superseded**, your contractor has
created a corrected version; it appears as soon as it is signed.

**Download PDF** gives you the protocol in its signed version as a PDF file.
Each download is noted on the protocol, so your contractor can see when it was
downloaded via the portal.

## What you do not see here

- Internal details such as fault details, defects or assignments to staff do
  not appear.
- Protocols that are not yet signed do not appear.
- You only see objects assigned to your company.

## Your objects elsewhere

Your objects can also be selected in other parts of the portal, provided
those areas are released:

- in **Register a return**, in the **Object** field, to send a device back,
- in an IT request under **Requests & orders**, in the **Affected object
  (optional)** field; this also requires **Objects** to be released.

## Limits

- You cannot create or change objects or reschedule appointments. If an
  object is missing or a detail is wrong, please contact your contact person.
- Report a fault on an object under **Tickets**, if that area is released.
- The **Objects** area only appears if it is released for your company.
