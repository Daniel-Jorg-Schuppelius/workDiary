Use the **light bulb** in the header to make a **wish**, report a **bug** or
ask a **question** about the app. The dialog includes the page you are on, so
it is clear what it is about. The same button opens your organization’s list
**Wishes for the app**.

## Voting and answers

- All members of your organization see the wishes and can agree with other
  people’s wishes – one vote per person. Nobody evaluates who votes how often;
  only the number per wish is shown.
- Sort by votes or date; filter by type, status or “Open only”.
- You can change your text yourself as long as the wish is **new**.
- Whoever holds the permission **Answer wishes for the app and forward them to
  platform operations** sets the status (new, under review, planned,
  implemented, rejected) and answers. You and everyone who agreed receive a
  notification.
- Each person can make at most 30 new wishes per day.

## Forwarding to platform operations

Your organization solves much on its own (setting, permission, training).
Anything beyond that is **forwarded to operations** by management. Before
sending, they remove customer and personal data from the text. Only this text,
the type, the page and the number of votes are forwarded – **without names**.
Operations see which organization the wish comes from so they can answer. Their
status and answer appear on the wish (“Answer from platform operations”); in a
self-hosted installation an **answer from the vendor** may appear as well.

This does not replace the help desk: requests from your customers stay there.
