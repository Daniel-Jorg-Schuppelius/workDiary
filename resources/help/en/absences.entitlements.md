The **vacation account** manages annual entitlement, carryover and remaining
balance per employee (MVP-413).

- **Create entitlement** per employee and year; “Create missing” fills all
  employees without an entitlement using the default value.
- **Carryover**: remaining days from the previous year with an expiry date
  (commonly 31 March). After expiry only the part actually taken counts.
- **Balance**: the request form, the approval view and the “Vacation & flex”
  report show entitlement, taken and requested days.
- **Overbooking only warns** — the decision stays with the approver. Special
  and unpaid leave do not count against the entitlement.
