The ISMS module bundles everything you need for a living information
security management system – without copying standard texts.
Certification itself is always performed by independent bodies.

The typical path:

1. **Scope**: every organization starts with the default scope
   **"Entire organization"** (cannot be deleted). Additional scopes
   separate SoA, risks and audits per site or process.
2. **Requirements & SoA**: load a standard catalog (e.g. ISO/IEC
   27001:2022, Annex A with 93 controls) or add your own requirements,
   then declare applicability per scope.
3. **Controls**: maintain standard-neutral controls and link them to
   requirements and risks.
4. **Risks**: record risks in the risk register, assess them in the
   5×5 matrix and treat them.
5. **Audits**: run internal/external audits with findings and
   corrective actions; approve management reviews.
6. **Certification**: maintain the conformity status per standard –
   the status **"Certified"** requires a certificate that is valid
   today and fully recorded.

Prerequisites: the **ISMS** module is part of the Enterprise plan.
Staff with ISMS read access can view the content; changes and scope
management require ISMS maintenance access.

The **audit readiness** dashboard (first entry in the ISMS section)
provides the overview: key figures per scope for high risks, overdue
assessment reviews, evidence gaps, overdue corrective actions, open
nonconformities and certificate dates – each tile links straight into
the corresponding register. The register pages (risks, requirements &
SoA, controls) additionally offer JSON/CSV exports with an explicit
data timestamp.

in use including end-of-life.
