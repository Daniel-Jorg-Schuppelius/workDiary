In the **supplier assessment** you rate your suppliers by criticality and
risk, record the required security requirements and contract terms, and
review them recurrently.

Typical flow:

1. **Assess supplier**: Either pick a supplier from your master data or
   enter a free-text name. Set criticality and risk rating and describe the
   service you procure.
2. **Security & contract**: Document the required security requirements and
   the contract terms — non-disclosure agreement (NDA), data processing
   agreement (DPA) and audit right. The DPA link is a **loose pointer** to
   data protection management; the case files are not merged.
3. **Review & findings**: Record the date of the last and the next review
   and capture findings.
4. **Maintain status**: from "Draft" through "Assessed" to "Approved"; or
   "Flagged" when follow-up is needed.

An overdue **next review** date is reported by the deadline scanner and
counts as an "unchecked supplier" in audit readiness while the assessment
is not approved.
