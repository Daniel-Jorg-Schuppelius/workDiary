If two-factor authentication is active for your account, a second step
follows once username and password are correct. You are only fully
signed in after confirming it. Until then your identity is merely
pending.

Depending on the methods you have set up, the following are available:

- **Authenticator app**: Enter the current one-time code from your app.
- **Email code**: Have a one-time code sent to your registered email
  address and enter it.
- **Passkey or security key**: Confirm the sign-in directly with your
  device or hardware key.
- **Recovery code**: If your regular method is unavailable, use one of
  your single-use recovery codes.

A short lockout also applies here after several failed attempts. You
manage the available methods and recovery codes in your account under
the security settings.
