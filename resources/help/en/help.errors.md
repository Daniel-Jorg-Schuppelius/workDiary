When WorkDiary cannot show a page, you see an error page instead of the
content. It states the reason, offers a way back and — where it makes
sense — "Report a problem". The request ID on the page helps your
administrators find the event in the log.

## Access denied {#forbidden}

Your account lacks the permission for this page or action. Permissions
depend on your role in the organization. If the page shows a specific
reason (for example a locked document), that reason applies. Contact your
administrators if you need access for your work.

## Page not found {#not-found}

The address leads to no content. Common causes: the record was deleted or
merged, the link is outdated, or it belongs to another organization. Open
the entry from the list or via search.

## Session expired {#session-expired}

The form was open for too long, or you signed out and back in in another
tab in the meantime. For security reasons it is then not saved. Reload the
page and submit the form again; copy longer texts to the clipboard first.

## Feature not in your plan {#plan-locked}

The feature belongs to a module your organization has not booked. Your
administrators see the included modules in the license overview, where a
plan change can be requested.

## Server error {#server-error}

An unexpected error occurred while processing. In this case your input was
not saved. Try again after a short while. If the error persists, report it
via "Report a problem" — the report contains the request ID used to find
the cause.

## Area under maintenance {#area-maintenance}

Your administrators have temporarily locked this area, for example for a
data migration. If known, the page states when the area will be available
again. All other areas remain usable.
