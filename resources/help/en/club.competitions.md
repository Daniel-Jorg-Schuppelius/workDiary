Individual, competition and shooting sports (athletics, swimming, gymnastics,
sport shooting, archery …) use the **disciplines of the sport profile**: each
discipline carries a unit (seconds, metres, points, rings) and the comparison
direction “lower is better” or “higher is better”. Age classes are calculated
by the club via the profile’s cut-off date; squads, lanes and training groups
are ordinary groups.

**Competitions:** A competition is a club event with sport, offered
disciplines, venue, organiser, entry deadline and optional entry fee. Judge,
timekeeper and range officer duties are event roles as in team sports.

**Entries:** An entry links a member to one or more disciplines; it makes the
member a participant (deadlines and seats apply). Members enter via the portal,
the leadership can add entries. An entry without a valid **start right** is
flagged “for review” — never silently rejected; the leadership clears it with a
note or the start right is added. The optional entry fee arises per discipline
as its own fee position in the billing month of the competition (account from
the fee assignment); withdrawn entries cost nothing.

**Start right:** A start right or licence is a documented check with validity
by the club administration, per sport or for all. The system does not
reconcile with associations.

**Performances and bests:** Performances are recorded manually with date,
discipline, value, optional placement and reference to training or
competition — unit and direction are frozen from the profile. A confirmed
value counts; the **best** per discipline (overall or per season) arises only
from confirmed values. Corrections are logged and remove the confirmation. No
automatic import from timing or competition software.

**Proof list:** The club configures attendance requirements (number of
confirmed attendances per period, optionally per group/department and event
kind). The proof list counts only confirmed, creditable attendances from the
attendance sheets as of a date and can be exported as CSV. The system holds
no legal thresholds, replaces no official certificate and contains no weapon
or ammunition management.

**Shooting sports:** Stands and lanes run through the facilities; the range
officer is a mandatory role — events of shooting groups without a range
officer show a notice.
