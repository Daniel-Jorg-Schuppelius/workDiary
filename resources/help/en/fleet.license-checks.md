**Driver licence checks** document the regular visual verification per driver
(MVP-417, owner liability).

- **Visual check**: who saw whose original licence and when — deliberately
  without photo upload (data minimisation).
- **Interval**: default 6 months, configurable per organisation.
- **Reminder**: due and overdue checks notify the driver and team leads.
- **Block**: drivers with an overdue check (or expired licence validity)
  cannot reserve vehicles until a new visual check is documented. Drivers
  without any first check are reported but not blocked.
