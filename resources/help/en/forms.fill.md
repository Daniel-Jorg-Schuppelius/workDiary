Forms let you fill in configurable checklists and records that your
organization has defined as templates. You find the overview under
**Daily operations** → **Knowledge & docs** → **Forms**.

Typical workflow:

1. Click **Fill out form** and choose a template – only templates in
   status “Active” whose period (**Valid from**/**Valid until**)
   includes today are offered.
2. **Fill in the fields**: depending on the template, for example Text,
   Multi-line text, Number, Date, Choice, Checkbox, Photo or Signature;
   mandatory fields are marked, some with help text and a unit. Some
   fields only appear once another field has a certain value.
3. The **Reference** results from where you start: via the **Forms** card
   on an order's detail page, the form is assigned to that order; there
   you only see templates whose assignment (entry type, customer) matches
   the order. Started from the **Forms** overview, the form has no
   reference. You do not pick a reference yourself.
4. **Submit** – the completed form is stored with a timestamp and your
   name.

Version safety: on submission, the template's field definition is frozen
as a **snapshot**. Display and print always use this snapshot – later
changes to the template (or even its deletion) never alter your completed
form retroactively. When viewing a form, **Print** and **Download PDF**
are available.

Permissions: anyone with the **Fill out forms** permission may fill in
forms. In the overview, people with the **See form templates**
permission (such as team leads) see all completed forms; everyone else
sees only their own.

Next steps: missing a field or a template? Contact your team lead –
they maintain the **Form templates**.
