Forms let you fill in configurable checklists and records that your
organization has defined as templates.

Typical workflow:

1. **Choose an active template** – only templates in status "Active"
   are available.
2. **Fill in the fields**: depending on the template text, multi-line
   text, number, date, select or checkbox; mandatory fields are
   marked, some with help text and a unit.
3. Optionally choose a **reference object** (work order, customer,
   asset, project).
4. **Save** – the submission is stored with a timestamp and your
   name.

Version safety: on save, the template's field definition is frozen as
a **snapshot**. Display and print always use this snapshot – later
changes to the template (or even its deletion) never alter your
submission retroactively.

Permissions: appropriately authorized staff may fill in forms. In the
overview, team leads see submissions within their area of
responsibility; other people see only their own.

Next steps: missing a field or a template? Contact your team lead –
they maintain the **form templates**.
