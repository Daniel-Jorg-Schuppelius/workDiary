The "Activity" page shows who changed what and when — across the organisation,
newest first, within the period selected in the header. It reads from the same
audit chain as the audit log and requires the same right; nothing is changed
in the process.

Filter by object type and person; an entry with a link opens the object. Which
fields changed is still shown in the audit log.

In the records of customers, end customers, suppliers and projects, the
"Change history" summarises the object's changes and events; the "Recent team
activity" tile shows the latest entries of the past two weeks.
