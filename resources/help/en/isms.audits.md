Plan and document internal, external and supplier audits here,
including findings, corrective actions and the management review.

Audit lifecycle: **"Planned" → "In preparation" → "In progress" →
"Report issued" → "Closed"**. From "Report issued" you may return to
"In progress" for report corrections. "Report issued" requires the
execution period and a result summary to be filled in.

Findings per audit:

- Kind: **"Major nonconformity"**, **"Minor nonconformity"**,
  "Observation", "Improvement".
- Status: "Open" → "Correction in progress" → "Effectiveness check"
  → "Closed".
- A finding can only be closed when all corrective actions are "Done"
  or "Effective" – for major/minor nonconformities at least one
  action must be **"Effective"**.

Corrective actions: "Open" → "In progress" → "Done" →
"Effective"/"Ineffective". The effectiveness decision requires a
mandatory **effectiveness note**; on "Ineffective" the finding
automatically returns to "Correction in progress". Fields: root cause
analysis, action plan, owner, due date.

Management review: records participants, inputs (audit results, KPIs,
risk situation), decisions and follow-ups. Status "Draft" →
"Approved" – **approved reviews are immutable** and can no longer be
deleted.

Permissions: viewing requires ISMS read access; changes require ISMS
maintenance access.

Next steps: audit results feed into the conformity status and into
finalized **audit packages**.
