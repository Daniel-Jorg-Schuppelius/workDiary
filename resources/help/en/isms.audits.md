Plan and document internal, external and supplier audits here,
including findings, corrective actions and the management review. You
find the pages under **ISMS** → **Suppliers & audit** → **Audits** or
**Management review**.

Audit lifecycle: **“Planned” → “In preparation” → “In progress” →
“Report issued” → “Closed”**; you set the next status via **Change
status**. From “Report issued” you may return to “In progress” for
report corrections. “Report issued” requires **Performed from**,
**Performed until** and the **Result summary** to be filled in.

Findings per audit (**Record finding**, only once the audit is “In
progress”):

- **Finding type**: **“Major nonconformity”**, **“Minor nonconformity”**,
  “Observation”, “Improvement opportunity”.
- Status: “Open” → “In correction” → “Effectiveness check” → “Closed”;
  from the effectiveness check you can return to “In correction”.
- A finding can only be closed when all corrective actions are
  “Implemented” or “Effective” – for major and minor nonconformities at
  least one action must be **“Effective”**.

Corrective actions (**Add corrective action**): “Open” → “In progress” →
“Implemented” → “Effective” or “Ineffective”. The effectiveness decision
requires an **Effectiveness note**; on “Ineffective” the finding
automatically returns to “In correction”. Fields: **Title**, **Root
cause analysis**, **Action plan**, **Owner**, **Due on**.

**Management review** (**Create review**): records the **Date**,
**Participants**, **Inputs** (audit results, KPIs, risk situation,
action status), **Decisions** and **Follow-up actions**. Status “Draft” →
“Approved” via **Approve** – approval records person and time;
**approved reviews are immutable** and can no longer be deleted.

Permissions: viewing requires the **See ISMS registers (risks, controls,
SoA)** permission; changes require **Manage ISMS (risks, controls,
catalogue import)**.

Next steps: audit results feed into the conformity status and into
finalized **Audit packages**.
