A damage case records an insurance or liability claim — from notification
to settlement. It always belongs to a record: a rental, a leasing contract,
a claim or a vehicle. The list under "Damage cases" shows all cases with
open and settled amounts and can be filtered by status, kind of damage and
record type.

## Report damage

On the record, "Report damage" opens the dialog. The kind of damage is
preset from the record (for example property damage for rentals, liability
for claims). Enter the course of events, time of damage, insurer, policy
and claim number and the estimated amount. Each case receives a number
(SCH-…).

## Track the settlement

The status leads from reported via submitted and under review to settled
or rejected; a rejected case can be submitted again. "Settled" requires the
settled amount. Settlement minus deductible gives the recovery. The history
shows every change; store correspondence and photos as attachments.
