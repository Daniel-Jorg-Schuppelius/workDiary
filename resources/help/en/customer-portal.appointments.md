On the page **Request an appointment** you choose a service and a free time window and thereby send your contractor an appointment request. The appointment is only fixed once your contractor confirms it. The page is available to you if your contractor has released online appointment booking to you. You then open it via **Request an appointment** in the portal menu.

## Finding time windows

1. Under **Service**, choose the service you want. Each service shows its duration in minutes.
2. Under **Date**, choose a day. If the field is left empty, the portal shows the earliest possible day.
3. Click **Show windows**.

Below the selection you see the description of the service, if one exists, and the free time windows of the day as buttons, for example “09:00–10:00”. At most twelve windows per day are shown, on a half-hour grid. Below that is the cancellation period of the service, for example **Cancellation is possible up to 24 hours before the appointment starts.**

The windows are based on the free time of your contractor's staff. If a service requires a particular qualification, only staff who hold it on the day of the appointment count. Each service also has a lead time: time windows that are too short-notice are not offered.

If **No windows are free on this day — please choose another day.** appears, choose another date and click **Show windows** again.

## Requesting an appointment

Click the time window you want. The request is sent immediately, without any further prompt. The portal reports **Appointment request sent — we will confirm bindingly.**, and the request appears under **My requests** with the status **requested**. Your contractor is notified of the new request.

If the window has meanwhile become too short-notice because the lead time is no longer met, the portal does not accept the request and states the lead time in hours. In that case choose a later window.

## What happens next

Your contractor reviews the request and decides:

- **confirmed** – you receive an email with the appointment as a calendar file (ICS) that you can add to your calendar. The appointment is scheduled as an order at your contractor; if the **Diary** has been released to you, you see it there with the status **Planned**.
- **declined** – you receive an email with the reason. The reason is also shown under **My requests** below the status.

Until the decision, the appointment is only a request.

## My requests

The list shows all requests of your access, the newest at the top, with:

- **Date** – date and time,
- **Service**,
- **Status** – **requested**, **confirmed**, **declined** or **cancelled**.

## Cancelling a request

You can cancel requested and confirmed appointments yourself with **Cancel**, as long as the cancellation period of the service has not expired. Your contractor sets the period per service; without its own setting it is 24 hours before the start of the appointment. Below the button you see until when you can cancel, for example **Can be cancelled until** with date and time.

After cancelling, the portal reports **Appointment request cancelled.**, the request has the status **cancelled**, and your contractor is notified. If the appointment was already confirmed, the scheduled order is cancelled as well; in the **Diary** it then shows **Cancelled**.

If the period has expired, **Cancellation period expired** is shown instead of the button. If it expires while the page is open, clicking shows **The cancellation period of … hours is undercut — please call us.** If your contractor is already working on the order, the portal does not accept the cancellation and reports **This appointment is already being processed — please call us.** In both cases, please call your contractor. Declined and already cancelled requests cannot be cancelled.

## Limits

- An appointment is only fixed after your contractor has confirmed it; the portal never books directly.
- You only see your own requests, not those of other people in your company.
- You cannot move an appointment. Cancel it within the period and request a new one.
