Projects combine customer, period, responsibilities, tasks, milestones,
time and billing rules.

Typical flow: create the project with customer and dates, assign
responsibility and status, plan tasks or recurrences, book services and
review progress on the project page.

Billing rules and project status affect which time and material can be
transferred. Close a project only after reviewing open tasks, bookings,
timesheets and billable lines.
