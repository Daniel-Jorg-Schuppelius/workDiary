"My start" is your **personal checklist for the first days** with WorkDiary.
It depends on your role: someone who records time sees different steps from
accounting or administration.

**Steps for everyone:** set up a second factor, choose your start page,
customise the dashboard and open the help centre.

**Steps by role:** for example record your first time entry, clock in and
out, record expenses, create your first order, create your first invoice as a
draft or get to know the financial reports. Administrators also find the way
to the organisation's setup checklist here.

**How to work through the list:**

1. "Open" takes you to the page where you complete the step.
2. WorkDiary recognises most steps as done by itself – for example as soon as
   your first time entry is booked or a second factor is set up.
3. Steps without a detectable sign are ticked off with "Done".

The header shows how many steps are completed.

**Hiding:** "Hide start" removes the tile from the dashboard. The list is
kept; you still find it in the user menu under "My start".

The personal start does not replace the setup of the organisation: that
checklist is handled by administration and covers master data, roles and the
first order.
