Registration creates a new organization together with its first
administrator account. You provide the organization name, your name, an
email address and a password. After submitting, the organization is set
up, your account is created as administrator and you are signed in
directly.

The new organization starts on the free plan. Additional features and
modules can be unlocked later through a license.

Registration is only available if it has been explicitly enabled in the
configuration. If self-registration is disabled, the page is not
reachable; new organizations are then created by the administration.
