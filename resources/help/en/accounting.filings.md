**The VAT return period** is the calendar quarter by law; filing is monthly if
last year's tax exceeded €9,000, and up to €2,000 the tax office may exempt a
business from advance returns altogether. The program keeps this setting as a
dated section, so it stays answerable which period applied in a past year. It
**never switches automatically**: the tax office decides, and the suggestion
shown is only an aid.

**The permanent deadline extension** moves filing and payment by one month.
Monthly filers pay a special prepayment of one eleventh of last year's advance
payments, due on 10 February; it is credited in the last return of the year.
Quarterly filers get the extension without it.

**The recapitulative statement** has its own deadline: the 25th day after the
reporting period. The extension does **not** apply to it — the most common
mistake in practice.

All deadlines are calculated, not maintained: if one falls on a Saturday,
Sunday or public holiday, it moves to the next business day. Only what has been
filed is stored.

> The program transmits nothing to the tax authorities. Filing still happens via
> ELSTER or your tax advisor; the reports are auditable previews for
> reconciliation.
