On a recurring item – such as an annual order – you leave a **note for next
time**: "Request donation receipt next year". The note belongs to the
**series**, not to the single item. It appears at the top of the next
occurrence of the same series and stays there until someone ticks it off.

## Leaving a note

In the **Notes for this time** section of the item, enter the text under
**Note for next time** and choose from which period it should apply (default:
the next one). The item's other notes do not carry over – only the note. You
can withdraw a note of your own that is still open.

## Ticking off a note

Open notes from earlier periods are shown at the top of the item, with the
name and the period they come from. **Tick off** with a short remark
("receipt requested") completes the note; afterwards it only appears as ticked
off on this item and no longer in later periods.

## Good to know

- **Skipped periods:** A note applies *from* its period. If a year is skipped,
  it appears at the next existing occurrence.
- **Same series only:** A note never moves into another series, e.g. to a
  different customer or a different kind.
- **Rights:** Whoever may see the item may see its notes; whoever may edit it
  may leave and tick off notes.
- **History:** Creating, ticking off with a remark and withdrawing are
  recorded in the audit log.

## Where notes exist

- **Orders with a period** in a series of customer, entry type and product group.
- **Recurring orders of a project** (monthly and yearly rules): the series is
  the recurrence rule. Daily and weekly rules have no notes because the period
  knows no weeks.
- **Recurring contract obligations** in the contract calendar: the series is
  contract and obligation kind, the period is the due date. When the obligation
  is completed, the newly created next obligation shows the notes.
- **Inspection duties of test equipment:** in the **inspection calendar** under
  **Record inspection** and on the items of an **inspection order**; the series
  is the inspection duty of the item of equipment.

Open notes are counted in the cockpit of the customer (contracts, recurring
orders), the project, the supplier (contracts) and the asset (inspection
duties).
