Products form the type level “manufacturer + model”: articles (trade/stock) and assets (devices in the field) of the same product meet here.

**Maintenance:** Manufacturer and model are unique per organisation; the display name is generated automatically otherwise. The product group anchors the existing classification at the type.

**Assignment:** The asset and article dialogs offer a product picker; assets prefill manufacturer/model from it. “Per model” reports then group by product instead of free text.

**Deletion:** Only removes the type — articles and assets remain.
