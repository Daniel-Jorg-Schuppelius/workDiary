The safety event register documents accidents, near misses, hazards and
defects in a traceable way – as a basis for audit, liability and
continuous improvement (Feature 013).

## Reporting an event

Use "Report event" to record an incident with its kind (accident, near
miss, hazard, defect), severity, time, location, affected person,
description and immediate action. Field staff may report; running the
register (editing, status, closing) stays with team leads and admins.

## Critical events

An accident or an event rated as critical immediately triggers a
notification to management, so serious
incidents are not missed.

## Status and closing

The status runs through *Reported → Investigating → Measures defined →
Closed*. Closing requires a root cause analysis. During or after closing
an open issue can be created as a follow-up (rework) measure.

## Qualifications and mandatory checks

Expiring qualifications and training are reported via the deadline
scanner. Mandatory safety checklists per
order type are modelled through procedure templates: a template's
applicability can target the order type, so critical activities can
enforce step sequences, proofs and four-eyes checks.

## Reporting

The safety analysis (Reports → Occupational Safety) shows events by kind
and severity over the period as well as open versus closed.
