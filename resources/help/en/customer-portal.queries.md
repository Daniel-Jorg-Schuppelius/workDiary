Under **Questions** you send your contractor questions or comments about
content released to you in the portal: an order in the **Diary**, an entry
under **Times** or a document under **Documents**. Every query remains
traceable for both sides with its reference, answer and timestamp.

## Ask a query

You start a query at the place it concerns:

- in the **Diary**, in the detail view of an order, via **Ask a query** in the
  **Queries and comments** section,
- under **Times** via the **Query** button at the end of a row,
- under **Documents** via the **Query** button next to **Download**.

The **Ask a query** form names the reference at the top, for example
“Order …”, “Time entry of …” or “Document …”.

1. Write your question in the **Your query or comment** field (at most 2000
   characters).
2. If needed, add up to five files under **Attachments (optional)** (PDF,
   images, Office, text, ZIP); the permitted size per file is shown below the
   field.
3. Click **Submit**. **Cancel** takes you back without saving.

After sending, the text and attachments can no longer be changed for
evidentiary reasons. Your contractor is notified, and the portal confirms:
**Your query has been submitted. You will be notified as soon as an answer is
available.**

## Queries and comments

The **Queries and comments** page lists your company's queries, the newest at
the top, 25 per page. Each card shows:

- the reference and the time of the query; if the record has since been
  deleted, it shows **(record no longer available)**,
- the status: **open**, **answered** or **closed**,
- the question with the name of the person who asked it and the attachments
  to download,
- your contractor's answer with name and time, once it is available.

When an answer is available, you also receive an email at the address of your
portal account. You also see the queries about an order directly in its
detail view in the **Diary**.

## Withdraw a query

As long as a query is **open**, you can take it back with **Withdraw query**.
It stays in the list with the status **closed**; the withdrawal is logged and
nothing is deleted.

## Limits

- Queries are only possible about content that is visible to your company in
  the portal. The **Questions** area does not make any further content
  visible.
- The **Query** and **Ask a query** buttons only appear if the **Questions**
  area is released for your company.
- You cannot reply directly to an answer; if needed, ask a new query about
  the same reference.
- Questions from your contractor about a request are not answered here but in
  the respective record under **Requests & orders**.
