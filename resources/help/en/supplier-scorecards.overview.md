Scorecards rate suppliers from real transaction data: on-time delivery (goods receipt vs. promised date), claim rate (from claim cases), price trend (from purchase order items) and ISMS risk classification.

**Score:** The metrics are combined into a weighted overall score; if a data source is missing it is shown as "no data" and the weights are re-normalised — no supplier is penalised for missing history.

**Ranking & drilldown:** The list sorts by score; the detail scorecard shows the metrics with charts and signed drilldowns into the underlying records.
