The **daily close** bundles everything that belongs to a working day on a
single page: attendance (punches), breaks, booked order and project times,
warnings and the daily balance (target, net, balance).

How to proceed:

1. **Review**: Open the page at the end of the day (menu "Daily work →
   Daily close"). Gaps and inconsistencies appear in the "Gaps & warnings"
   section.
2. **Top up**: Book missing times via **"Book time"**. Punches themselves
   can only be changed through a correction request.
3. **Close**: Once no ⛔ warnings remain, close the day with
   **"Close day"** (also via `Ctrl+Enter`).

⛔ warnings block the close: an open punch clock, unallocated attendance
(> 5 min) or an unmet statutory break. ⚠ hints (e.g. balance beyond ±2 h
or more than 10 h of work) do not block.

After closing, the day is locked for you. If you need a change, request a
review via **"Request correction"** (reason, at least 20 characters) —
team leads or admins decide on it. After approval only bookings can be
changed; punches stay locked.

Days in an already approved month are fully locked; any change there goes
through the monthly approval.
