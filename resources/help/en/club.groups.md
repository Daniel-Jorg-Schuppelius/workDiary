Groups model your training and club units: children's, youth, beginner or
open groups, optionally assigned to a department. Each group has a leader,
an optional capacity and an admission mode — directly by the leader or as a
request with approval.

**Age criteria:** Minimum and maximum age are inclusive; an empty field means
open. The age is checked on the day of joining the group. Without a date of
birth the result is "review required" — there is no tacit admission. A member
may belong to several groups at once, but only once to the same group.

**Admission and requests:** "Admit member" checks criteria, capacity and
duplicate assignment. Only the club administration may allow an exception
from the criteria, always with a reason; it is logged. Requests do not
reserve a place — the check runs on approval.

**Change proposals:** A daily check compares all active assignments with the
limits of their group. If a member exceeds the maximum age on their birthday
or you change the limits, a proposal is created — never an automatic removal.
If exactly one suitable, free group exists in the same department, it is
suggested as the target. The leadership confirms with an effective date (the
old assignment ends the day before) or dismisses the proposal. "Check criteria
now" triggers the check immediately.

**Group leaders:** Whoever leads a group sees and operates only that group:
admissions, requests, endings and proposals of their own group. Group master
data and exceptions remain with the club administration.
