Data processing agreements (DPA) and non-disclosure agreements (NDA) are managed as dedicated contract kinds in contract management — always with a customer as contracting partner. In the customer file, the **Agreements** section shows which contracts exist and how far the signing has progressed.

**Workflow:** Create the agreement (from the customer file via “Add agreement”), upload the contract PDF and annexes in the **Documents** section of the contract file and create a **version** from them. The version binds exactly these file versions, both parties with name and function, and the confirmation text. “Release for signature” freezes it; from then on a later document version changes neither the view nor the record.

**Two signing routes:** With “Create signature link” or “Send signature link”, the customer side receives a time-limited one-time link (seven days), sees the version including annexes and signs in the browser — drawn or, keyboard-accessible, by typing the name with an explicit declaration. Alternatively the customer uploads a signed PDF; a contract received by post or e-mail can also be recorded internally. Uploaded evidence counts as a signature only after **review** (version, completeness, parties, signatures); a rejection requires a reason, after which new evidence can be submitted.

**Countersignature and completion:** The organisation side countersigns in the logged-in area. Once all defined parties are fulfilled, the version is “fully signed” and open links are blocked. The **completion record** (PDF) and the **download package** (originals, annexes, received PDFs, record) are then available. For the customer, create a separate, revocable **retrieval link** (30 days) or release the version in the customer portal.

**Permissions:** Signature requests, countersignature and replacement require the permission “Customer agreements: versions, signature links, countersignature”; reviewing evidence is a separate permission. A general customer read permission is not sufficient. The contract can only be activated with a fully signed version; bound document versions cannot be deleted.

**Note:** Signing in the browser is a simple electronic signature without identity verification and not a qualified signature under eIDAS. You provide the contract texts and annexes yourself.
