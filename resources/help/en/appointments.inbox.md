Portal customers request appointments for **curated services** — dispatch
decides here. **Two-phase, non-negotiable:** only the confirmation creates
the dispatch entry; no customer writes into the duty roster directly.

## Services

Only what is created here is bookable: duration, **lead time** (how
short-notice requests may be), **cancellation period** (until when the
customer may cancel) and buffer. Deactivating removes the service from the
portal immediately.

## Slots

The offered windows come from dispatch idle time — externally only as
**anonymous time windows**: no names, no utilisation. A slot is bookable or
not; why is none of the customer's business.

## Deciding

**Confirm** creates the entry and binds the request to it. **Decline**
requires a reason — it goes to the customer. If the customer cancels within
the period, the request disappears from the inbox.
