**Controls** are standard-neutral: they describe HOW your organization
implements something – without a standard reference. They are linked
to standard requirements and risks via mappings (several each).

Typical workflow:

1. **Create a control**: title, description, owner.
2. Set the **implementation status** (mandatory): "Open", "Partial",
   "Implemented" or "Not applicable".
3. Record **evidence** (reference to a document/proof).
4. Link it to **requirements** (SoA) and **risks**.

Why standard-neutral? One control (e.g. "central patch management")
can satisfy several requirements from different standards and mitigate
several risks at the same time – you maintain it only once.

Permissions: ISMS read access allows viewing. Creating and editing
require ISMS maintenance access.

Next steps: linked controls appear in the printable SoA view and are
included in finalized **audit packages**.
