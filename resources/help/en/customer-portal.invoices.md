Under **Invoices** you will find the invoices that have been issued for
your company. The overview gives you a compact summary of all documents
on file.

The list is sorted by invoice date, with the newest invoice at the top.
For each entry you see the **number**, the **date**, the **status** and
the **amount** including currency. The status tells you the processing
stage an invoice is currently in.

When there are several pages, use the page navigation at the bottom to
browse. This view is provided for your reference and traceability; the
invoicing itself is handled by the company you commissioned.
