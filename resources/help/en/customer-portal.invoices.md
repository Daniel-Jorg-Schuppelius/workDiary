Under **Invoices** you see the invoices your contractor has issued for your company, together with their current payment status. You can pay open invoices online right here if your contractor offers this.

## The invoice list

The list shows for each invoice:

- **Number** – the invoice number,
- **Date** – the invoice date,
- **Status** – the payment status,
- **Amount** – the invoice amount with currency,
- on the right, where applicable, the button **Pay online**.

The newest invoice is at the top. With more than 25 invoices you page through the list using the pagination at the bottom. Drafts your contractor is still working on do not appear – you only see issued invoices. The invoice documents themselves cannot be opened or downloaded on this page.

### The statuses

- **Issued** – the invoice has been issued, no payment has been recorded yet.
- **Partially paid** – part of the amount has been received.
- **Paid** – the invoice has been settled in full.
- **Cancelled** – the invoice has been cancelled and no longer needs to be paid.

## Paying online

You only see the button **Pay online** if your contractor has connected a payment provider and the invoice still has an open amount, i.e. has the status **Issued** or **Partially paid**. Credit notes, cancelled invoices and pro forma invoices cannot be paid online.

1. Click **Pay online** next to the invoice.
2. You are forwarded to the payment page of the payment provider. The amount to pay is the amount still open – for a partially paid invoice, only the remainder.
3. Complete the payment with the provider. You then return to a confirmation page.

The confirmation page shows one of these notices:

- **This invoice has been paid. Thank you!** – the payment has been recorded.
- **Thank you! The payment is being processed; …** – the payment provider has not yet confirmed the payment.
- **This invoice cannot be paid online. …** – please contact your contractor.
- **Online payment is not possible at the moment. …** – try again later or pay the amount by bank transfer.

## What happens next

As soon as the payment provider confirms the payment, it is recorded against the invoice: the status changes to **Paid**, or to **Partially paid** if an amount remains, and the button **Pay online** disappears. Your contractor therefore sees the payment as well; you do not need to inform them separately. Bank transfers only appear once your contractor has recorded them.

## Limits

- The page is for viewing. Only your contractor issues, changes or cancels invoices.
- Please clarify questions about an invoice directly with your contractor.
- If your contractor bills you through a billing account, you find the monthly overview under **Billing**.
