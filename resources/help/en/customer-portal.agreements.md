Under **Agreements** you see the data processing and non-disclosure agreements that exist between you and us or are currently out for signature — per agreement with **version**, **status** (ready for signature, partially or fully signed, replaced) and the date of full signature.

The signing itself takes place via the signature link you receive from us — not in the portal. Via the link you see the version including annexes, can download all files and sign in the browser or upload a signed PDF.

Once a version is fully signed and **released** by us, the **completion record** (PDF) and the **download package** with contract, annexes, received signed PDFs and record are available here. Until release, the row shows “not released yet”.
