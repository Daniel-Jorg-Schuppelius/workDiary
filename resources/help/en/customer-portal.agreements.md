Under **Agreements** you see the agreements your contractor concludes with you and has signed: **Data processing agreement (DPA)**, **Non-disclosure agreement (NDA)** and **Rental terms (equipment rental)**. You see which version is out for signature and how far signing has progressed, and after completion you download the documents.

## The list

The list shows for each version of an agreement:

- **Contract type** – DPA, NDA or rental terms,
- **Title** – with the contract number below it,
- **Version** – for example “Version 2”, where applicable with “effective from” and a date,
- **Status** – the signing state, after full signature with its date,
- **Documents** – the downloads, as soon as the version has been released.

The newest version is at the top. Drafts and withdrawn versions do not appear. If there are no versions, **There are no agreements at the moment.** appears.

### The statuses

- **Ready for signature** – the version is out for signature.
- **Partially signed** – at least one signature is present, others are still missing.
- **Fully signed** – all signatures are present.
- **Replaced** – a newer version has superseded this one.

## Signing

You do not sign in the portal itself. For that, your contractor sends you a personal signature link. Via this link you

- review the version with contract and annexes and can download every file,
- sign under **Sign in the browser**: confirm name and function, declare authority to represent and consent, sign and submit,
- or, under **Upload signed PDF**, upload the printed and signed contract as a PDF. The signature then only counts after your contractor has reviewed it.

Signing in the browser is a simple electronic signature without identity verification; it does not replace a qualified electronic signature. If the link is invalid, ask your contact person for a new one.

## Downloading documents

As soon as a version is fully signed and your contractor has released it in the customer portal, the **Documents** column offers:

- **Completion record (PDF)** – the record of the completed signing,
- **Download package (ZIP)** – contract, annexes, the signed PDFs received and the completion record in one file,
- the individual files of the version, each labelled **Contract** or **Annex**.

Until release, the column shows **not released yet**. Replaced versions remain downloadable if they had been released. Downloading the completion record and the download package is logged with the contract. Independently of the portal, your contractor can also send you a separate retrieval link for the documents after completion.

## Limits

- Only your contractor can create, change, withdraw or release agreements.
- Other contracts with your contractor do not appear here.
