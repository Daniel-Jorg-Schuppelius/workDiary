You do not re-enter orders with a **period** (such as “Income tax 2025”) for
the next period: **Create next period** creates the order with one click.

## What is copied

- customer, entry type and product group,
- responsibility, review requirement and named reviewer,
- the procedures of the order as new runs.

**Notes** for the next period appear in the new order automatically. Content
and notes, times and the result are not copied.

## Due date from the customer deadlines

The due date of the new order comes from the **customer deadlines**: the
earliest date among the customer’s deadlines in the new period that the rule
set assigns to the entry type and product group of the order. Waived
deadlines do not count. Without a matching deadline the due date stays empty.

## Single or in bulk

- **Single:** on the order via **Create next period**.
- **In bulk:** in the order book via **Next period in bulk**, choose entry type
  and period. The preview shows which orders will be created and which already
  exist; only then do you create them.

## No duplicates

There is at most one order per customer, entry type, product group and period.
If it already exists, the existing order is named instead of creating a second
one. Cancelled orders do not count.
