Three rates, three questions — with an example (month with 160 h target):

- **Utilization** = tracked ÷ target time. 120 h tracked → **75 %**.
  Is the workload sufficient? Target comes from the work schedule
  (holidays/vacation do not count as target).
- **Billable rate** = billable ÷ tracked time. 90 of 120 h billable →
  **75 %**. Does time flow into paid work?
- **Realization** = invoiced ÷ billable time. 72 of 90 h on invoices →
  **80 %**; the gap is write-downs, forgotten positions or not-yet-billed
  time. Requires local invoicing — otherwise the report honestly shows "—".

## Reading the charts

- **Bullet per person**: bar = actual, vertical line = target from
  Admin → Targets (per person, falling back to org-wide). Click opens the
  person's monthly report.
- **Box plot per month**: box = middle half of all people (Q1–Q3), line =
  median, whiskers = range. A wide box means utilization is unevenly
  distributed. Downward outliers are usually planning or tracking gaps —
  **not a performance rating**.
- **Monthly trend**: clicking a point narrows the report to that month.

## What to do with it

- Utilization persistently below target → review sales/planning first,
  don't start with staff cuts.
- Billable rate declining → identify internal time sinks (the entry type
  analysis helps).
- Realization below ~90 % → review the billing run and price discipline
  (billable time exists but doesn't reach invoices).

This report is deliberately **not a ranking**: sorted by name, team total
first.
