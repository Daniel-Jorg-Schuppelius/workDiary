Three rates, three questions:

- **Utilization** = tracked time ÷ target time (from the work
  schedule). Is the workload sufficient?
- **Billable rate** = billable ÷ tracked time. Does time flow into
  paid work?
- **Realization** = invoiced ÷ billable time. Does the work end up on
  an invoice? (Requires local invoicing; otherwise "—" is shown
  honestly.)

The bullet charts compare each person against the **target value**
from Admin → Targets (per person, falling back to org-wide). The box
plot shows the monthly distribution — downward outliers are usually
planning or tracking gaps, not a performance rating. This report is
deliberately **not a ranking**: sorted by name.
