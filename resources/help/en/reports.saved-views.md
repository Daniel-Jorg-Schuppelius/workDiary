Saved views turn a configured report into a named one-click entry point:
open the report, set the filters, paste the URL from the address bar here
and give it a name — done.

Views start out personal. "Share" makes them visible to the whole
organisation: everyone looks at the same figures (such as the official
monthly reporting) instead of rebuilding filters individually.

Only internal report pages can be saved; creators and administrators can
share, unshare and delete views.
