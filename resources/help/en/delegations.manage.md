If someone is absent, a colleague takes over that person's **assigned work**
for a while – without any record being reassigned. The absent person remains
responsible; when the deputyship ends, nothing needs to be reworked.

## What a deputyship does

- **Work queue:** The deputy additionally sees the assigned orders, project
  tasks and service tickets of the person covered in their work queue, marked
  "Covering for …". Anything they already have appears only once.
- **Notifications:** Messages about assigned work (new assignment, comment,
  customer reply, deadline) also go to the deputy during the deputyship, with
  the note "Covering for …".
- **Note in the header:** The deputy reads "You are covering for …" at the
  top; the person covered reads "Covered by …".

## What a deputyship does not do

- **No additional rights.** Approvals, reviews and anything tied to a
  permission are still checked against the acting person.
- **Nothing personal.** Times, absences, the personnel file and private notes
  of the person covered remain invisible. Only the person covered and
  management see the reason (leave, sickness).
- **No chains and no other organisation.** Someone being covered cannot cover
  for others at the same time; deputyships only exist within your own
  organisation. A person has at most one deputy at a time.

## Creating a deputyship

- **From approved leave:** In the **Vacation** tab of **Leave & sickness**, an approved request
  shows the button **Create deputyship**. Period and reason are prefilled, and
  the person's named deputy is suggested. You handle your own leave yourself;
  other people's leave only with the permission **Create and end deputyships
  for others**.
- **From a sick note:** Management (same permission plus access to sick notes)
  creates the deputyship on the sick note in the **Sick leave** tab.
- **Freely:** Via **Deputyships › Create deputyship**, for example for a
  training course.

The dialog shows what the deputy takes over – only the number per area. A
deputyship starts today at the earliest. Without an end date, it applies until
someone ends it.

## Ending a deputyship

A deputyship ends automatically on the last day of the period. **End
deputyship** in the list ends it immediately; the person covered, the deputy
and management may do so. Creation and end are recorded in the audit log.

The rule of the named deputy for leave decisions remains unchanged.

## Working as a deputy

Whatever the assignment allows the represented person, the deputy may do
during the deputyship as well: open assigned orders and move them through the
life cycle (accept, start, complete), open the person's confidential service
tickets, plan and complete their project tasks. In the order list **Only mine**
and for tickets **Assigned to me** also show the represented person's items;
the work queue additionally lists their running procedure runs. Rights are
always checked for the acting person: whoever may not plan tasks or review
orders may not do so as a deputy either.

## Notification and ending with the absence

The deputy is notified when the deputyship is created – with name and period,
never with the reason. If the underlying leave is cancelled or rejected, or the
sick note is cancelled, the deputyship ends immediately.

## Handing over permanently

If an absence is permanent (parental leave, move to another team), hand the
work over for good: **Members › Hand over responsibilities** opens the same
handover section as offboarding, just without leaving. For each area –
assigned orders, open tasks, service tickets, running procedure runs, idea
maps – choose a successor of its own or none. The person's audit log records
how many items per area went to whom. This requires the right **Create and end
deputyships for others**.

Without access to the member list, use **Deputyships › Hand over
responsibilities**: choose the person, then the successor for each area;
afterwards you are back on the deputyships page. The responsibility for
knowledge pages passes to a successor the same way – including pages in the
trash.

Hand over individual orders in the **order list**: tick the orders, choose the
new person in the bar and click **Change responsibility**. Orders you may not
edit, and orders for which the new person lacks a required qualification, are
skipped.
