Every KPI in the reports is clickable. The drilldown shows the
**concrete work orders** that went into the number – with all active
filters applied.

How to read the drilldown:

- **Columns** show customer, entry type, status, planned/actual
  duration.
- **Sorting** defaults to date, descending.
- **Export** as CSV or PDF creates a snapshot including the filters and
  the generation timestamp in the file name.

Important: the drilldown respects your tenant boundary and the order
statuses you are allowed to see. Whatever you cannot see in the list
does not count in the aggregated report either.
