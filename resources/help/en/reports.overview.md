Reports aggregate operational data by period, employee, team, project,
resource or financial context. Visible values depend on permissions,
the active organization and current filters.

Review the period and filters first, then totals and unusual values. Use
details or drilldowns to trace figures back to their source records.
Exports use the current filter state.

Reports are not a separate data source. Corrections belong on the
original order, time, absence or master-data record. Apply need-to-know
access to personal and financial reports.
