Here you manage the members of your organization: name, personnel number,
email and the role (administration, user, accounting). When a member is
created an initial password is set that the person must change at first
login. The list is tenant-scoped and shows only members of your own
organization.

The personnel and payroll block (tax ID, social security number, health
insurance, tax class, employment type and period, and the compensation
model with hourly wage or flat rate) may be maintained not only by
administrators but also by HR and management. Anyone holding only this
permission can view the master data but cannot change identity, role or
password.

A person's working-time model is maintained separately via the
working-time editor. It defines target hours and the valid period and forms
the basis for flextime and working-time evaluations. Creating and deleting
complete members remains reserved for administration; the number is limited
by the license's user quota.
