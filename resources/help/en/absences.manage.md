Absences affect duty planning, target-versus-actual time and month
approval. Leave follows a request and approval workflow; sickness is
recorded with its period and required evidence.

Employees enter the complete period. Responsible staff review overlaps,
coverage and remaining entitlement, then approve, reject or request a
correction.

Health data requires particular protection. Record only necessary
information, limit attachments to required evidence and follow access
and retention rules.

## Sick leave and continued pay

In the dialog **Record sick note** you first choose the **Type**: **Initial
certificate** for a new incapacity for work or **Follow-up certificate** if a
certificate is extended – then you select the **Preceding sick note**.

With the type **Initial certificate**, administrators and people with the right
**Manage sick leaves** additionally see the field **Continuation of illness
from**. Select the earlier sick leave of the same person there if the health
insurer confirms that it is the same illness. The days of illness then count
towards the same continued pay entitlement. Without this confirmation, **None —
new illness** stays selected.

This is how WorkDiary calculates continued pay under § 3 EntgFG:

- In the default setting the entitlement is six weeks, i.e. 42 calendar days of
  incapacity for work. Only sick days count, never the working days between two
  sick leaves.
- Sick leaves that overlap, follow on seamlessly or are follow-up certificates
  form one sickness case – even if a new illness starts during an ongoing one.
- A new illness that only begins after working days starts with the full
  entitlement.
- For a continuation of illness, a new entitlement for the same illness arises
  if the person – in the default setting – was not unable to work because of
  this illness for six months, or if twelve months have passed since the start
  of the first incapacity.

The work list shows the status in the **Sick leave** area under **Continued
pay** with the days used and remaining. **Expected end** only appears while the
sick leave is ongoing; once the entitlement is used up, the date since which it
has been exhausted is shown instead. The report **Sicknesses** shows the status
per person. All figures are a guide, not legal advice.

Days with a sick note have no target time, just like vacation: **Working-time account**, **Work balance**, **Attendance** and **Plan/actual** do not count them as a minus. Cancelled sick notes count nowhere, not even in time accounts.
