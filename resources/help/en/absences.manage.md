Absences affect duty planning, target-versus-actual time and month
approval. Leave follows a request and approval workflow; sickness is
recorded with its period and required evidence.

Employees enter the complete period. Responsible staff review overlaps,
coverage and remaining entitlement, then approve, reject or request a
correction.

Health data requires particular protection. Record only necessary
information, limit attachments to required evidence and follow access
and retention rules.
