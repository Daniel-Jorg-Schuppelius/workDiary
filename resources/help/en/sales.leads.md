A **lead** is a prospect **before** customer status — from a referral, the
web, a trade fair or a call. The lead file holds contact, source and need;
qualification runs through the **communication notes** with follow-up, just
like on a customer.

## The pipeline

**New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted / Discarded.** A discarded lead can
be requalified; a converted one lives on as a customer.

## Converting — with duplicate check

Before creating, the file shows **possible existing customers** (name,
email). Linking is the equal option — a second customer master through the
back door is exactly what duplicate clean-up laboriously repairs later. The
lead file remains after conversion and points to the customer: the
acquisition history belongs to the acquisition.

## Retention

Leads are personal data **without a contract**. Non-converted leads are
anonymised **6 months after the last contact** — via the retention scan of
the privacy module (review queue, no silent deletion). The pipeline metric
(source, status, period) stays, the person disappears.
