## Purpose and background

Time tracking records when and on what work was done — as the basis
for time accounts, payroll and billing customers. Systematic
recording is mandatory for businesses; WorkDiary meets this with a
time clock, time entries and traceable corrections.

## Requirements

- **Start** (date + time) — while the stopwatch runs, the end stays
  open.
- A **project** or **customer** for the assignment.
- An **activity category**.
- For time accounts: a stored work schedule.

## Recommended workflow

1. Start via **"New entry"** in the top bar or via the **stopwatch**
   (also available as a dashboard tile).
2. Assign the running time to a customer, project or order — the
   assignment can be changed later.
3. Stopping creates the time entry; check it in the week view and
   split it if needed.
4. Enter **breaks** separately via the day-close view, not on the
   time entry.

## Practical example

A technician starts the stopwatch in the morning on "server room
maintenance · Müller GmbH", drives to the next customer at noon and
books anew there. In the evening every hour sits on the right order —
the day close only adds the break.

## Common mistakes

- **"Estimating" the end** instead of letting the stopwatch run —
  corrections cost more time than stopping.
- **Writing breaks into the time entry:** breaks belong in the day
  close, otherwise time account and billing are wrong.
- **Booking everything onto a collective customer:** without a real
  assignment the time cannot be billed later.

## Effects and next steps

Every entry flows into time accounts, reports and the open times of
billing. Next: check entries in the week view, maintain the day close
and use the designated correction path instead of re-creating
entries.
