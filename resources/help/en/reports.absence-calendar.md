The absence calendar shows all approved absence periods of a year as bars on
a yearly axis — overlaps within a department are visible at a glance. Use
the filters to pick the **Year** and, if you see all people, a **Team**.

Without a further right you only see your own row. Administrators and people
with the right **See all vacation requests** see all people of the
organization. The absence reason of other people – such as vacation, special
leave or sick leave – is only visible to those who additionally hold the right
**View sick leaves**, and to administrators. For everyone else, other people's
absences appear neutrally as “absent”; you always see your own absence
reasons.

The switch **Anonymise absence reasons** only appears with both rights or for
administrators. It shows other people's absence reasons neutrally as “absent”
for these people as well, e.g. for a printout on the notice board. The same
applies to the absence card and the exports.

Clicking a name opens the personal absence card: the year calendar with
weekends, public holidays and absence codes, plus the vacation account block
(entitlement, carryover, taken, planned, remaining) and the statistics of
calendar and effective working days per absence reason.

CSV and PDF export the absence periods as a list with calendar days and
effective working days.
