"My personnel file" shows the documents your organisation keeps about you –
such as the contract, amendments and certificates. The file is
**confidential**: apart from you, only your organisation's personnel-file
group can see it.

**Reading:** Every document can be downloaded; earlier versions are listed
under "Versions". The list shows category, validity, retention period and the
date of the last change.

**Read confirmation:** If a document carries the note "Read confirmation
pending", HR is asking for your confirmation. With "Read" you confirm that you
have read the document; the date is recorded. A new version requires a new
confirmation.

**Submitting a document:** You hand in your own documents – for example a
certificate or a proof – with "Submit a document". HR reviews it and either
adds it to your file or rejects it with a reason. Until the decision, the
submission stays at the top of the list; a rejection shows the reason.
Accepted documents appear as a document in the file.

**Notifications:** You are informed as soon as a read confirmation is
requested or a decision has been made about your submission.

You can neither change nor delete documents in the file. If something is
wrong, contact HR.
