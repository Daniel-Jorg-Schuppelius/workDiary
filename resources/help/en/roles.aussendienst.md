As field service you record times, work orders, tours and expenses on
the go – all on your own assignments.

Typical flow:

1. **Start time tracking** (stopwatch or "New entry"), pick
   project/customer and activity category.
2. **Document the order**: content, photos and attachments directly in
   the order's case file.
3. Create a **protocol** on the order and have the customer **sign** it
   on site (touch signature or signature link).
4. Maintain **tour and travel log** (vehicle/energy log), record
   expenses.
5. **End of day**: enter breaks, close open time entries.

What you can do:

- Create, edit and delete your own **time entries** – until they are
  approved; afterwards every correction goes through a **correction
  request**.
- Create and edit your own **work orders** (diary).
- Maintain **tours, travel logs and vehicle logs**, view vehicles.
- **Request vacation** and view your own **time account/flex**.
- **Read** customers and projects – master data is maintained by
  accounting or team leads.

Not in your profile: approvals, reports across other people, master
data and administration pages.
