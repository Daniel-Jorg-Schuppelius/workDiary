Videos in learning units are **processed** after upload: the file is converted
into a web-ready format and provided in several resolutions. While this runs,
the unit shows the processing state; playback is possible only afterwards.

You can provide **subtitles** in two ways:

1. **Upload your own file** (WebVTT). It applies immediately and unchanged.
2. **Have them generated automatically.** The result is a **draft** and is not
   delivered until someone has reviewed and released it.

The review is mandatory by design: automatic transcription often mishears
technical terms, names and numbers — and a wrong subtitle is worse than none,
because it pretends to be reliable. During review you can edit the text,
discard it entirely, or release it.

**Accessibility:** For mandatory courses, subtitles are not optional. Plan the
review before you publish a course.
