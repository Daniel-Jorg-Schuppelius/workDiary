Holidays and non-working days feed into planning and time management. For
the selected year, the overview shows the statutory holidays together with
the entries you maintain yourself.

When creating an entry, choose the recurrence mode: once on a fixed date,
yearly on the same date, or relative, such as "third Monday of the month".
For relative days you provide weekday, week and optionally the month; for
fixed days you provide the date.

Duplicate fixed holidays are rejected. Entries that are no longer needed can
be deleted; the selected year only controls the displayed resolution, not
the set of stored rules.
