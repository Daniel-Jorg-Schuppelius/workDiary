Customers raise queries about a submitted item (protocol, acceptance, order)
through the portal or the **signature link**. Every query lands here as a
traceable entry and triggers a notification to the responsible role.

Working with queries:

- **Open** queries are listed first. Use **Answer** to store an internal
  reply; it becomes visible to the customer through the same link.
- Use **Close** to mark a resolved query without answering it (e.g. clarified
  by phone).

**Approval and rejection:** Through the signature link the customer can also
**reject** the item. A rejection requires a reason; reported defects are
automatically recorded as **open issues** (source "customer rejection",
customer-visible) on the affected order or protocol. Both approval and
rejection are logged tamper-evident with a timestamp.
