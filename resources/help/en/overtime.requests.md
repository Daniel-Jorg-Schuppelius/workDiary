An overtime request lets you get extra work beyond the working time frame
approved instead of silently booking it to the flex account. The request
states the affected day, the extra minutes and the operational reason.

The overview lists your own requests with their status. Open requests can
be withdrawn. You are notified about the decision.

Team leads decide the requests in a dedicated inbox. An approval documents
the operational reason in an audit-proof way and automatically acknowledges
the matching "working time frame exceeded" finding from the clocking
plausibility check — the case is thereby considered resolved. Time accounts
keep calculating unchanged; the request is the governance layer for the
extra work.
