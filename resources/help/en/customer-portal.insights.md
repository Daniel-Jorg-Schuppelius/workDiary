Under **Insights** you see key figures and trends for the areas your contractor has released for your company – per calendar year. The area is a separate release; if it is missing from the menu, your contractor has not released it (yet).

## Choosing the year

At the top you choose the **year**. Only years with data are offered; the current year is selected when you open the page.

## Times

Appears when the **Times** area is released. You see the **hours** of the year and the previous year with the change in percent, the number of **entries** and **projects**, plus:

- **Hours per month** – twelve columns, the previous year as a dashed line,
- **Hours per project** – the ten largest projects, the rest as **Other**,
- **Hours per month by project** – stacked columns (only if individual entries are shown to you).

The figures are the same as in the **Recorded times** list; the year limit applied there applies here too. Clicking a column opens the list with the matching month or project.

## Invoices

Appears when the **Invoices** area is released. Per currency you see the number of **invoices**, the **gross invoice amount**, the **open amount** and the **overdue** amount (open and past due), plus:

- **Invoice amounts per month** – invoices as columns, **credit notes** hatched separately, never netted,
- **Open amount at month end** – what was issued and not yet paid at the end of each month; partial payments count as open until fully paid,
- **Payment status per month** – a table with count, amount, credit notes and open remainder.

Drafts and cancelled documents are not counted. Clicking a month opens the invoice list with that month.

## Billing account

Appears when your contractor bills you through a billing account: the key figures of the latest month (**Total**, **Billed**, **Previous month**, **Open**) and the **balance at month end** as a line. Clicking a point opens the month.

## Tickets

Appears when the **Tickets** area is released: **opened** and **resolved** tickets of the year per month and the **average resolution time** in days – the latter only from five resolved tickets per year, so that single cases do not distort the picture.

## Reading the charts

Every chart has a table with the same values below it, also for screen readers. Hovering over a column shows the exact value; the legend toggles series on and off. Without JavaScript the chart remains readable as an image with table.

## Limits

- You only see areas released for your access, and within them only what the lists show.
- There is no export of the insights as PDF or spreadsheet; invoices and evidence are downloaded in their areas.
