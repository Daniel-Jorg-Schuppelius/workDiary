A **personal workspace** is your own selection of menu entries. It shows up in
the switcher (top of the page) next to the predefined focus views — only for
you, nobody else sees it.

## What it does — and what it doesn't

A workspace only **hides**. It never changes permissions, never unlocks a
module and never deletes anything. Hidden entries stay reachable through
global search, deep links and **All functions**.

That is why the editor only offers what you may see anyway — and the server
checks your selection against the same list again when saving.

## Creating one

1. Give it a **name** (it appears on the tile in the switcher).
2. Optionally set an **icon** and an **order** — the order decides where the
   tile sits in the switcher.
3. Click a section, group or entry in the catalogue on the left to add it. A
   whole section brings everything inside it along.

## Setting the order

Two equally valid ways:

- **Drag** the handle on the left of the row.
- **Without a mouse:** tab to the handle and move the row with *Arrow up* /
  *Arrow down* — or use the ▲/▼ buttons in the row.

## Switching and cleaning up

Pick the workspace in the switcher like any other focus view; in the list the
arrow button switches to it directly. If you delete the workspace you are
currently in, the view falls back to **Show everything**.
