Foreign customers are the end customers of your own clientele: a company
(customer) in turn has customers you work for. They are thus distinct from
your own regular customers and are each linked to one customer. Foreign
customers serve, among other things, as a target for imports when naming the
end customer in the project title is not enough.

In the overview you filter by customer, search across the master data and
separate active from archived entries. The detail view shows the linked
projects and the change log. Creating and editing happen in a dialog.

A foreign customer can be archived instead of deleted once projects or
external references depend on it. If an end customer comes directly to you,
you can promote it to a full customer: a customer is created from the
contact data, all projects are reassigned, and the foreign customer is
archived.
