Open issues are tasks, defects or points to clarify that your contractor records for your company, your orders or your projects. On the **Open Issues** page you see the issues your contractor has explicitly made visible to you, together with their processing state.

## How to open the page

On the **Overview**, click the tile **Open Issues**. The number on the tile states the issues that are not yet closed. The tile appears if your contractor has released this area to you. With the same release, the menu also shows **Known errors**; that list is described in the help topic on tickets.

## What an issue shows

Each issue is shown as a separate card with:

- the **status** as a coloured label,
- the severity: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Critical**,
- a category, if assigned,
- **Due by** with a date, if a deadline has been set,
- the title and, where applicable, a description,
- **Assigned to** – who is working on the issue at your contractor (a dash if nobody has been assigned yet),
- **Closed at** with date and time, if the issue is closed.

### The statuses

- **Open** – the issue has been recorded and is not yet being worked on.
- **In progress** – someone is working on it.
- **Blocked** – work cannot continue at the moment.
- **Reopened** – an issue that had already been done has been taken up again.
- **Done** – the issue is completed.
- **Won't do** – the issue is deliberately not being carried out.

## Order and paging

Issues that are not yet closed are at the top, closed ones below; within both groups the most recently created issue comes first. With more than 25 issues you page through the list using the pagination at the bottom. If there are no issues, **No open issues released for you.** appears.

## Limits

- The page is for your information. You cannot create, comment on, change or close issues; your contractor does that.
- You only see issues your contractor has explicitly released to you. Internal issues remain hidden.
- The list does not show which order or project an issue belongs to.
