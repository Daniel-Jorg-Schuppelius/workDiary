Under **Open Issues** you see matters and items related to your company
that have been explicitly released to you. Only issues linked to one of
your jobs, projects or job-book entries are shown.

Each issue shows its **status** (e.g. open, in progress, blocked, done),
its **severity** and – where available – a **category** and a **due
date**. Below that you find the title and a description. Open issues are
listed first, completed ones follow with their closing time.

You also see who is responsible for handling the issue. This view is for
your information; the issues themselves are processed by the company you
commissioned. Use the page navigation to browse longer lists.
