The **Onboarding checklist** guides a new organisation into productive
operation in **fixed, defined steps**. You open it via **Open
onboarding** in the **Set up onboarding** widget on the dashboard.
Completed steps are detected automatically from the existing data – you
do not have to tick anything off manually. The **Progress** bar shows
how many required steps are done.

Required steps (cannot be skipped):

- **Complete organisation details** (name, timezone and language of the
  organisation)
- **Choose branch profile**
- **Verify roles** (at least one person with the Administrator role and
  one with a working role such as Employee, Team Lead, Field Service or
  Accounting)
- **Create first customer**
- **First project or job**

Recommended steps: **Choose feature scope**, **Set up workspaces**,
**Invite first users**, **Verify classifications**, **First time
entry**, **Sign first protocol** and **Backup heartbeat** (a successful
backup within the last 26 hours).

You can leave out recommended steps with a **Reason for skipping** and
**Skip**; the reason is stored in the **Audit log** and shown next to the
step. **Dismiss widget** hides the widget on the dashboard for the whole
organisation; it no longer appears afterwards, and the checklist has no
menu entry of its own.
