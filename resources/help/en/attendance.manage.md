The time clock records attendance start and end independently of
individual project bookings. Clocking out closes the open attendance
period and makes it available to attendance and working-time reports.

Before finishing work, verify that active stopwatches and attendance are
both stopped. Differences between attendance and booked time are
possible, but should have a clear business explanation.

Do not hide incorrect bookings with compensating entries. Use the
correction workflow so the change and its reason remain traceable.

**Check-in by QR code or NFC:** If a check-in code hangs at a location or
vehicle, scanning it with your own phone opens a page showing the point's name.
After signing in you confirm clock-in or clock-out; the booking records the
point so it stays traceable where you clocked. A button tapped twice does not
accidentally book the opposite. If the point has a radius, the page asks for your
position once – it is only used for the check and is not stored.

**Kiosk:** At a shared workstation a tablet can serve as a time clock. You hold
your badge to the reader; the display confirms clock-in, clock-out or break.

Forgot your badge? "Clock with PIN" works with your personnel number and PIN,
provided the administration has given you a PIN. After five failed attempts it
is locked for 15 minutes.

**Evening reminder:** If you have been clocked in for at least eight hours at
7 p.m., you get a reminder – once per clock-in. Anyone starting a shift in the
evening is not reminded.
