Two-factor authentication protects your account in addition to the
password. During sign-in, you confirm your identity with a second,
independent method.

Available methods:

- **Authenticator app**: Scan the QR code with an app and confirm setup
  with the displayed one-time code.
- **Email code**: A one-time code is sent to your registered email
  address.
- **Passkey or security key**: Uses secure authentication on your
  device, smartphone or hardware key.

After the first method is activated, **recovery codes** are displayed.
Store them securely outside WorkDiary. Each code can be used only once
when your regular method is unavailable.

You can register multiple methods and remove individual factors. If
your organization requires two-factor authentication, the last active
factor cannot be removed and protection cannot be disabled completely.

Important: Never share the QR code, manual secret, one-time codes or
recovery codes with anyone.
