The calendar shows your appointments in a monthly view. On-call shifts,
emergency duties and work log entries that have a scheduled time are
merged together. For work log entries, opening one takes you straight to
its detail view.

By default you only see your own entries. With administration rights you
can switch to a team view and additionally narrow it to a specific
person to review their on-call shifts and appointments.

This calendar is the interactive view within WorkDiary. If you want to
subscribe to your appointments in an external calendar application, use
the ICS calendar subscription in your profile.
