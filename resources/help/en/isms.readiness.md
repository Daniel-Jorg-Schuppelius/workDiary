The **maturity / readiness assessment** derives a **reasoned
self-assessment** of your audit readiness per scope from the existing ISMS
registers.

For each domain (SoA coverage, risk management, evidence, audits &
corrections, operations, suppliers) it forms a maturity level as a traffic
light and a score (0–100) — with the triggering signals as the rationale.
From the domains it produces an overall "internally audit-ready?" verdict
plus a list of the blocking gaps.

> **Important:** The result is solely a reasoned self-assessment and a
> recommendation. WorkDiary **never** automatically claims conformity and
> does not certify. Conformity assessment and certification are carried out
> exclusively by an independent certification body.

Use the assessment to close the red domains before an internal audit. The
metrics are the same as in the audit-readiness dashboard, here condensed
into a maturity level.
