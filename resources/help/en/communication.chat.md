Chat supports quick internal coordination in channels and direct
conversations. Replies, reactions, mentions and pinned messages keep
decisions discoverable in their conversation context.

Use chat for short-lived coordination. Information that permanently
belongs to a customer, project or order should also be recorded on the
corresponding business record.

Do not share credentials or unnecessary personal data. Edited or deleted
messages may remain traceable depending on permissions and audit rules.

**Poll with an end:** When creating a poll in the chat you optionally set when voting ends. After that, the poll accepts no more votes.
