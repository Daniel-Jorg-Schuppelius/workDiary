The working-time account compares target time, eligible actual time and
absences. Month approval closes a reviewed period for further changes
and exports.

Before submission, review open bookings, breaks, leave, sickness and
approved corrections. Team leads or payroll review differences and
approve the month or reject it with a reason.

Direct changes are restricted after approval or export. Later errors use
correction requests so balances and the audit trail remain reproducible.

**Own month and team:** Nobody decides on their own month, administrators
included – unless nobody else in the organisation holds the approval right;
the audit log then records the self-approval. Whoever may see months and
correction requests for the team only sees the members of their own teams.

**Account balance:** Target, actual and balance of the current month count only completed days up to yesterday, like the stored account, the tile and the terminal. The account balance includes the carry-over from the previous month. Approving the month freezes it in the account; only reopening recalculates it.
