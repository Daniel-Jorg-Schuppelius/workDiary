The working-time account compares target time, eligible actual time and
absences. Month approval closes a reviewed period for further changes
and exports.

Before submission, review open bookings, breaks, leave, sickness and
approved corrections. Team leads or payroll review differences and
approve the month or reject it with a reason.

Direct changes are restricted after approval or export. Later errors use
correction requests so balances and the audit trail remain reproducible.
