The customer analysis shows per customer:

- number of work orders in the period
- booked time (billable / internal / travel / rework, separately)
- open items and rework rate
- non-billable time as an early indicator of margin risks

Filters:

- **Period** (weekly/monthly grid)
- **Order type** and **status**
- **Employees** (for training/coaching needs)

Clicking a row opens the **drilldown** to the order list with the same
filters – so every KPI stays traceable down to the individual order.
