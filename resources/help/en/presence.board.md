The presence board shows at a glance who is currently in the building, who
is on an external assignment and who is absent for the whole day — intended
for reception and switchboard to route calls and visitors quickly.

For privacy reasons, the board never shows absence reasons: absent people
appear neutrally as "absent". The feature can be disabled per organisation
and is off by default; administrators enable it in the organisation
settings.

For emergencies (evacuation) there is a separate, protected emergency
attendance list with more detail.
