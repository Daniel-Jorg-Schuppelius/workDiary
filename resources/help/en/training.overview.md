Training management keeps track of **which courses exist**, **who owes
them** and **by when** — the records themselves are still created as
instructions in the safety register.

## Course catalogue

A course describes its title, provider (internal or external), duration,
validity in months, legal basis (for example the annual instruction
required by occupational safety law) and optionally its cost. Costs are
informational only — no posting is created. Every course carries versions,
so it stays provable which content was taught.

## Requirement matrix

The requirement matrix defines which role or work area (team) owes which
course. Every assignment turns into a plan entry per employee when the plan
is refreshed; its first date follows the configured lead time. If an
assignment is removed, only plan entries without a record disappear —
everything backed by a record stays.

Installing an industry profile brings matching suggestions along
(construction: fall protection and scaffolding, haulage: load securing,
care: hygiene, electrical: the annual electrical instruction).

## Records

The record is the attendance of an instruction in the safety register. If a
course and optionally a course version are referenced there, the plan
entries of the attendees count as fulfilled and the next due date follows
from the course validity. Nothing is captured twice — removing an
attendance reopens the plan entry immediately.

## Due dates

The deadline scanner reports every plan entry whose lead window is reached
to the person concerned and escalates to the team lead. Nothing is blocked
by this: blocking notices for assignments still come from the employee
qualification status.

## Report

The training report shows the compliance rate per team, role and course on
the reference date plus due and overdue entries. The export as CSV, Excel
or PDF serves as competence evidence in audits.

## Deliberately out of scope

No learning platform and no course content, no tests or effectiveness
reviews, no competence matrix with skill levels and no health data —
occupational medical checkups stay in the safety register.
