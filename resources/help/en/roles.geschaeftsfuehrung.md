As management you have the executive view: read access to practically
all areas, without operational write permissions.

Typical flow:

1. **Dashboard and reports**: customer analysis, entry type analysis,
   drilldowns down to the individual order.
2. **Times and utilization**: timesheet and time overviews, the team's
   time accounts (flex).
3. **Finance**: view invoice and billing states.
4. **Compliance**: review the audit log and access history; follow the
   ISMS and conformity state.

What you can do:

- **Read everything**: orders, times, customers, projects, invoices,
  duty plans, absences, reports.
- **Export reports** (CSV/PDF).
- View the **audit log** and access history.

Deliberately not included: write permissions. Changes (approvals,
master data, configuration) go through team leads, accounting or the
admin – keeping the segregation of duties clean.
