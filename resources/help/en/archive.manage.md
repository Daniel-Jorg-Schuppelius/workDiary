The archive shows completed or archived work outside the active work
list. Filters and date ranges help locate historical entries.

Archiving does not automatically complete every connected process.
First review open time, tasks, documents, acceptances and billable
lines. Historical content remains available for evidence and reporting.

Restoration requires the appropriate permission and a permitted status.
Do not use it to bypass completed billing or approval workflows.
