In the customer portal you see the matters your contractor handles for your company – for example orders, invoices, documents or tickets. Which areas are available to you is decided by your contractor for your company. Anything that has not been released appears neither in the menu nor on the start page nor in the search.

## The Overview start page

After signing in, the **Overview** opens. At the top the portal greets you with **Welcome** and your name; below it is the name of your company.

Below that there may be announcements from your contractor, each with subject, date, time and text. These are, for example, notices about disruptions (shown for a few more days after resolution with the addition **all clear**), product recalls that affect your company, or a sustainability excerpt with the greenhouse gas emissions of a period.

## The tiles

For each released area the start page shows a tile with a number. Clicking the tile opens the area.

- **Diary entries** – the number of your orders.
- **Time entries** – the number of time entries you can view. The tile only appears if individual time entries are shown to you.
- **Invoices** – the number of your issued invoices.
- **Open Issues** – the number of open issues released to you that are not yet closed. You can reach the **Open Issues** page only through this tile.
- **Open requests** – your ongoing requests and orders. If items are waiting for a reaction from you, the tile has a coloured border and states their number (“Of which … await you”).
- **Subscriptions & licences** – the number of subscriptions and licences in your list.

If no area has been released for your access yet, instead of the tiles you see the notice **No areas have been released for your access yet.** In that case, please contact your contact person at your contractor.

## The menu

The header always shows **Overview**, **Search**, **Help**, **Profile**, **Security** and **Sign out**. In between, only the areas released to you appear:

- **Diary** – your orders with photos, material and protocols,
- **Objects** – your objects,
- **Documents** – documents released to you,
- **Times** – recorded times for your projects,
- **Invoices** and, if your contractor bills you through a billing account, **Billing**,
- **Tickets**, **Service catalog** and **Known errors**,
- **Complaints** and **Register a return**,
- **Rental** and **Rental request**,
- **Subscriptions**,
- **Agreements**,
- **Requests & orders**,
- **Request an appointment**,
- **Training**,
- **Questions**.

**Training** appears if your contractor offers training in the portal; there you only see courses that your contractor has released for customers.

**Help** opens the help topic for the page you are currently on; if there is none, the list of the portal's help topics. There, too, you only see topics for areas that have been released to you. **Profile** and **Security** are described in the topic “Access & Security”. **Sign out** ends your session.

## Searching

With **Search** in the header you search the released areas. Enter at least two characters under **Search term** and click **Search**. The search looks:

- in **Invoices** for the invoice number,
- in **Orders** for the title of the order,
- in **Documents** for the title of the document,
- in **Tickets** for ticket number or title.

The results are grouped by these areas, at most ten per area, each with a date. Clicking an order or a ticket opens its detail page; clicking an invoice or a document opens the respective list. If the search finds nothing, **No results for “…”.** appears.

## What you can do in the portal

- You only see matters of your own company.
- Most areas are for viewing. Where you can do something yourself – such as confirm photos, pay invoices online, request appointments or ask questions – is described in the help topic of the respective area.
- You cannot change yourself which areas are released. Your contractor decides that; if you are missing an area, please contact them.
