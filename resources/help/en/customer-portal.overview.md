Welcome to the customer portal. After signing in, the start page greets
you with your name and your company's name and shows four metric tiles
that give you a quick overview at a glance.

The tiles show how many **job-book entries**, **time records** and
**invoices** are on file for you, and how many **open issues** are
currently released to you. Each tile also acts as a shortcut: a single
click takes you straight into the matching detail view.

In the portal you can only view items that are assigned to your company
and have been released to you. You can read the displayed data, but you
cannot change it. The menu also gives you access to managing your login
and your two-factor security.
