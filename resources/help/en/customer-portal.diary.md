In the **Diary** you find the orders your contractor handles for your company – planned, ongoing and completed. For each order you open the case file with released photos, the material used, protocols and communication. There you can confirm or dispute photos.

## The order list

The list shows for each order:

- **Date** – the start of the order,
- **Title** – a click opens the case file,
- **Status** – the processing state.

The most recent order is at the top. With more than 25 orders you page through the list using the pagination at the bottom.

### The statuses

- **Planned** – the order has been created, work has not started yet.
- **Accepted** – the order has been accepted, work has not started yet.
- **In progress** – work is being done on it.
- **Waiting for response** – the order is on hold until a response is received, usually from your side.
- **Waiting for material** – the order is on hold until material has arrived.
- **Completed** – the work is done.
- **Signed off** – the work has been accepted.
- **Invoiced** – the order has been billed.
- **Cancelled** – the order will not take place.

## The case file of an order

At the top you see title, date and status, on the right the button **Case file as PDF**. These sections follow:

- **Photos** – the photos and files your contractor has released to you: photos as a small preview image with their file name, other files with their name. Clicking the image or the name downloads the file. Internal photos do not appear.
- **Material** – the material recorded for this order with **Name** and **Quantity**.
- **Protocols** – signed protocols that your contractor has released for you, with title, status and date, each with **Download PDF** (see below).
- **Communication** – released notes on conversations and messages with subject, time and text.
- **Queries and comments** – only if queries have been released to you (see below).

If a section is empty, it shows a notice such as **No approved photos.**

## Confirming or disputing photos

Next to each photo you have not yet confirmed there are two buttons:

- **Confirm** – you confirm the photo. Afterwards it shows **Confirmed on** with the date. The confirmation cannot be withdrawn; each person with their own portal access confirms individually.
- **Dispute** – the field **What is wrong?** opens. Describe the problem in at least 3 and at most 2000 characters and click **Send**.

A dispute is sent to your contractor as a query about this order, and your contractor is notified. The message **Complaint submitted — we will get back to you.** confirms that it was sent. You receive the answer by email at your login email; if queries have been released to you, it also appears in the section **Queries and comments**.

## Downloading protocols

The portal only shows signed protocols; drafts and protocols under review appear once they are signed. The status reads **Signed**, for older protocols also **Archived** or **Superseded**. With **Superseded**, your contractor has created a corrected version; it appears as soon as it is signed.

**Download PDF** gives you the protocol in its signed version as a PDF file. Each download is noted on the protocol, so your contractor can see when it was downloaded via the portal.

## Case file as PDF

**Case file as PDF** downloads a PDF file with title, date and status of the order, the released photos including confirmation date, the material and a list of the signed protocols with title, status and date; you download the protocols themselves one by one with **Download PDF**. Communication and queries are not included.

The link behind the button is valid for 24 hours and also works without signing in – so you can pass it on to colleagues. If it has expired, open the case file again; a new link is created.

## Queries about the order

If queries have been released to you, the case file shows the section **Queries and comments** with your previous questions and the answers from your contractor, each with name and time. With **Ask a query** you write a new question about this order; the details are described in the help topic on queries. Internal comments of your contractor never appear here.

## Limits

- In the diary you cannot create, change or cancel orders. If released, you request new services under **Requests & orders**.
- You only see photos, protocols and notes that your contractor has explicitly released to you. Protocols appear only once they are signed.
