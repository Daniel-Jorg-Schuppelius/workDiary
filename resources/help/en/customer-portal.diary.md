In the **job book** you can see the work and activities documented for
your company. Each entry represents a recorded job and serves you as a
traceable record of the services delivered.

The list is sorted by date, with the most recent entry at the top. For
every entry the **date**, the **title** and the current **status** are
shown. This lets you see at a glance what was worked on and how far a job
has progressed.

When there are many entries, use the page navigation at the bottom to
browse the list. The entries are provided for your information only;
changes are made exclusively by the responsible staff.
