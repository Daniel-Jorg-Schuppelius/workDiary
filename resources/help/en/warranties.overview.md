The period list keeps warranties per project — both your own towards the
client and those of the subcontractors.

**Basis:** The period runs from acceptance. VOB/B yields four years, the
German Civil Code five; a deviating agreement is possible but requires a
reason — otherwise the system later holds a period nobody can explain.

**Subcontractors first:** The list highlights where a subcontractor's period
ends before your own. That is exactly the gap in which you are still liable
while the subcontractor can no longer be held to account.

**Defects:** A reported defect is recorded with its date; the period stays
visible until it is closed. The end of a period is the moment to return a
warranty guarantee or to release the retention.

## Changing a period, notice of defect and acceptance date

**Edit:** Open and notified periods can be changed via the pencil icon — for
example when the end date moves after a suspension or restart of the period.
The same rules apply as on creation: without an end date the legal basis
calculates it, and a deviating end needs a reason. Linked open retentions take
the new end as their release date; linked guarantees report their return after
the new end. Closed periods stay unchanged.

**Record notice of defect:** If a defect is notified within the period,
**Record notice of defect** sets the period to *Notified* and optionally links
the claim case in which the defect is handled (selection only with the claims
module active and read permission). The period continues unchanged; enter a
new end via **Edit** if needed. While the notice is open, a linked guarantee
does not report its return. **Close** also ends a notified period.

**Reminders:** Notified periods stay in the reminders — the notice does not end
the period, and the reminder still comes before its end. In the comparison with
subcontractor periods, a notified own period counts as well; a notified
subcontractor period is considered secured and no longer triggers a warning.

**Acceptance date:** Selecting an acceptance protocol in the dialog prefills
its date as the **Start** — the period starts on the day of acceptance. A
start date you entered yourself is kept.
