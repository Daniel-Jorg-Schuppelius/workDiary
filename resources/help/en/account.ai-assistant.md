On this page you decide whether an **AI assistant** – such as Claude or
ChatGPT – may access WorkDiary on your behalf. It appears when you set up the
connection to WorkDiary in the assistant.

**What the assistant may do:**

- **Reading** is always part of the approval: customers, projects, orders,
  quotes, invoices, open items, time, appointments, key figures and search –
  with your permissions. The assistant never sees more than you do.
- **Creating drafts** is a checkbox you can clear: quote and invoice drafts,
  creating customers, moving assignments. Issuing and sending stay with you.

**Check the target:** The page names the address the access goes to after
your approval. If it does not belong to a known assistant, a warning appears
and you have to confirm that you set up the connection yourself. Only approve
if you have just started the process yourself – otherwise a stranger gains
access to your data. If in doubt, choose "Deny".

**Revoking:** Afterwards the access is listed under Profile → API tokens and
can be revoked there at any time.

**Requirement:** The organisation must allow AI assistants – in the
organisation settings under "AI assistants (MCP)". If this is switched off,
no new connection is possible; existing accesses no longer provide tools and
cannot be renewed.
