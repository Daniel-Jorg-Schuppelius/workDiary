The register keeps official and organisational permits in one place — from
excavation or special-use permits and event approvals to internal
clearances. Which permit types are typical depends on your organisation's
domain; the configured industry profile provides suitable defaults.

**One file per permit:** Each permit is its own record with title, permit
type, responsible authority or body, reference number, application date and
validity period (from/until). Conditions and ancillary provisions are kept
in the notes so they stay visible during execution. The status follows the
lifecycle: required, applied for, granted or rejected; expired permits are
marked accordingly.

**Link to operations:** A permit can be assigned to an event or job. This
makes it visible right on the event whether the necessary approvals are in
place before work starts.

**Evidence:** The official notice or proof is attached directly to the
record as a document (PDF, images or Word documents up to 25 MB). During
inspections or queries, the decisive document is immediately at hand without
searching through filing systems.

**Expiry monitoring:** The overview sorts by the valid-until date by
default, so the permits expiring next appear at the top. A permit counts as
overdue when its deadline has passed without it being granted — it is
clearly highlighted so that re-application or follow-up can be initiated in
time. Filters by status and a search across title, authority and reference
number help with larger inventories.

The register documents the permit situation but does not replace legal
review: whether a specific activity requires a permit and which conditions
apply is decided by the responsible authority or qualified counsel.
