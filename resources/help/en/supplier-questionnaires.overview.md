With the self-assessment you request information from suppliers, for
example on certificates, sustainability or data protection. You maintain
the questionnaires under "Suppliers → Self-assessment"; you assemble the
questions from fields as with form templates. A validity in months defines
how long an accepted self-assessment applies.

## Send a request

In the supplier record, "Request self-assessment" opens the dispatch. The
supplier receives a link by email that is valid for 30 days and fills in
the questionnaire without signing in. The questions are frozen per
request: later changes to the questionnaire do not alter given answers. An
open request for the same questionnaire is withdrawn when you send it
again.

## Review answers

You see submitted self-assessments in the request. Accept them or return
them for rework. The internal supplier assessment in the ISMS remains
separate.
