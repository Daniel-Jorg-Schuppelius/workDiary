Under **Custom fields** you extend customers, orders, assets, articles and
projects with fields your organisation needs in addition — for example a
utility customer number, an inspection interval on an asset or a cost centre
on a project.

For each subject you maintain one field list: label, type (text, number,
date, choice, checkbox, scale, …), required flag, options, unit and value
range. The fields appear in the subject's form as the section "Additional
fields", on the detail page and in exports; text values feed the search.

Notes:

- **Deactivate instead of delete**: once values exist, switch the field list
  off — stored values are kept and can be shown again later.
- **Keys derive from the label** and stay stable even if you rename the
  label later.
- **Industry profiles** ship suitable fields; your own fields are never
  overwritten.
- File, photo and signature fields are not available here — use
  attachments and forms for those.
