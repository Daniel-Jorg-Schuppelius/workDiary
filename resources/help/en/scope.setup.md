The **What would you like to do?** dialog sets up the compact start for your
organization. You choose goals, the dialog shows right away which workspaces
and modules follow from them, and applies everything in one step. You can
skip it at any time: without a choice, your package's defaults apply.

## Goals

- **Customers and orders** and **Time** are always included. They bring the
  workspaces "Customers & Orders" and "My Working Day".
- **Team planning** adds "Team & Planning" with duty plan, scheduling and
  team reports. The goal is only available if your license includes
  planning; otherwise the dialog explains how to obtain the extension.
- **Quotes and invoices** switches on the commercial view: the workspace
  "Quotes & Invoices" and the quick actions Quote and Invoice. Without this
  goal the invoice pages remain reachable; only the compact workspaces hide
  them.

## What the dialog writes

- the **feature scope** (active modules) within your license,
- the **offered workspaces**, a default and a suggestion per role,
- the **start page per role**,
- the scope mode **deliberately limited**: modules that are added to your
  license later stay deactivated until you activate them on the feature
  scope page.

Personal settings of your staff (workspace, hidden menu items, start page)
are not changed. No data is deleted; everything can be adjusted on the
feature scope page.
