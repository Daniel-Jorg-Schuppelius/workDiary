Serial numbers track individual devices across their entire life
cycle. Each serial number is unique organization-wide (duplicate
guard) and moves through the states created, in stock, reserved,
shipped, returned, blocked or scrapped. It is linked to article,
variant, warehouse and – on delivery – the customer supplied and the
associated delivery.

The overview can be filtered by status and serial number. The detail
page shows the device passport with origin, any manufacturing order,
shipping link and status – useful for warranty and fraud checks, for
example to identify whom a device was actually shipped to.
Verification checks an entered serial number for authenticity and
current status.

Blocking marks a device as unavailable (for example lost, stolen or
recall) and can be lifted again. Scrapping is a terminal status and
irreversible. Viewing and verifying are possible with the inventory
read permission; blocking, unblocking and scrapping require the
inventory posting permission.
