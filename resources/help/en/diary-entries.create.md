A new work order entry is created via **"New entry"** in the top bar or
through a quick action on the dashboard.

Capture when creating:

- **Customer** (required) and, if applicable, **project**
- **Entry type** (e.g. maintenance, incident, installation)
- A short **summary** in 1–2 sentences
- Optionally the **planned duration** in minutes

Status transitions happen in the detail modal – there is no bulk update
from the list. This keeps the audit trail clean and prevents accidental
mass rollbacks.
