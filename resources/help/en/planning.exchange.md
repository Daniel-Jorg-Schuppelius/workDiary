Use the shift exchange to give away one of your shifts or swap it for
another. The plan only changes after the team lead has approved it.

Workflow:

1. You request to give away or swap one of your shifts. Optionally you
   name a preferred partner and an offered shift in return.
2. The preferred colleague can accept the exchange (for an open
   give-away the first matching colleague takes the shift).
3. The team lead reviews the approval. The system automatically checks
   whether the new assignment is compatible with rest periods, maximum
   working hours, overlaps and absences.
4. Only on approval does the shift assignment change — for a real swap
   both shifts change.

A request can be withdrawn as long as it has not been decided.
