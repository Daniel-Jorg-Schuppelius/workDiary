Under **Rental** you see your current and completed rentals with devices, period and status.

With **Rental request** you request devices for a period. If direct booking is enabled, you book available devices immediately; otherwise the lessor confirms the request. The price shown is an estimate from the price list.

If rental terms need to be signed, you will find them under **Agreements**.
