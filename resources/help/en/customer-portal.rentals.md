In the rental area you see the equipment your company rents from your
contractor and confirm its handover. With **Rental request** you request
equipment for a period or book it directly if your contractor has enabled
that. **Rental** and **Rental request** are released separately; you only see
what is enabled for your company.

## My rental devices

The **Rental** menu item opens the **My rental devices** page with all rental
transactions of your company, the newest at the top, 20 per page:

- **Number** – a click opens the transaction.
- **Devices** – the devices of the transaction.
- **Period** – start and end of the rental.
- **Status** – the state of the transaction:
  - **Draft** – created but not yet firmly reserved, for example after an
    accepted request.
  - **Reserved** – the devices are firmly reserved for your period.
  - **Handed over** – the devices are with you.
  - **Overdue** – the rental end has passed and the devices are not back yet.
  - **Returned**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**.

If the rental request is also released, the **Submit rental request** button
at the top right takes you straight there.

## Rental transaction and confirming the handover

The **Rental transaction …** detail page shows the status and the period with
times, followed by the devices with their state: **Planned**, **Handed
over**, **Returned** or **Swapped**.

Under **Handovers** each handover is listed with **Time**, the
**Accessories** handed over and the **Confirmation** column. Check that the
devices and accessories have arrived complete, then click **Confirm
handover**. Afterwards the column shows “confirmed on …” with date and time.
The confirmation cannot be undone.

Internal cost, risk or maintenance notes do not appear here.

## Requesting equipment

The **Rental request** page shows the devices your contractor has released for
requests from the portal.

1. Under **Desired period** choose the start and end with date and time and
   click **Check availability**.
2. The **Requestable equipment** table shows for each **Device** the **Device
   group**, the **Availability** in the period (**free** or **booked**) and,
   under **Price (net)**, an estimate from the price list marked “approx.”.
   Without a period it shows **Choose period**.
3. Under **Device or device group** choose a specific device or a device
   group. For a group, your contractor assigns a suitable device.
4. If needed, enter under **Purpose / note (optional)** what you need the
   equipment for.
5. Click **Send request**.

The period must not start in the past, and the end must be after the start.
The availability is a rough indication; the rental only becomes binding once
your contractor confirms it.

## What happens after the request

Your contractor is notified and decides on the request. You receive the
decision by email at the address of your portal account. **My requests**
shows your latest requests with **Period**, **Device** and **Status**:

- **requested** – not decided yet. Use **Withdraw** to take the request back;
  its status then reads **withdrawn**.
- **accepted** – your contractor has accepted the request and created a
  rental transaction that appears under **Rental**.
- **declined** – your contractor's reason is shown below.

Decided requests can no longer be withdrawn.

## Book now

If your contractor has enabled direct booking, a **Book now** button appears
next to **Send request**. It immediately and bindingly reserves a specific
device, provided it is free in the period; device groups cannot be booked
directly. The booking appears under **My requests** with the status **Booked
directly** and as a rental transaction with the status **Reserved**. Your
contractor is notified and sends you the handover documents.

## Rental terms

If rental terms have to be signed for the rental, you find them under
**Agreements**. The most recently signed version is recorded on the rental
transaction.

## Limits

- You cannot extend the rental period, swap or return devices in the portal;
  please contact your contractor for that.
- The prices shown are net estimates from the price list, not an invoice.
