The takeoff sheet records quantities in a traceable way — on an order, a
project or a bill of quantities. It calculates with the formulas of
REB-VB 23.003; the same values go into the GAEB quantity survey (X31).

How to record lines:

- **Add line** opens the formula: rectangle or cuboid, triangle, trapezoid,
  circle, sum, mean or a free formula with operators.
- **Factor** stands for identical parts; a negative factor deducts, for
  example a door from the wall area.
- **Templates** of your trade (from the industry profile) prefill formula,
  unit and factor.
- **Quick entry** also works offline; the line is calculated and saved on
  the next connection, photos follow.

After **completing** the sheet it is locked. You then transfer the
quantities once per target:

- **as a quote** or **as a draft invoice** — the invoice receives the
  takeoff PDF as a document,
- **as progress** of the assigned bill of quantities items.

Completed takeoffs without a transfer appear in the document chain under
“To bill and to follow up”.
