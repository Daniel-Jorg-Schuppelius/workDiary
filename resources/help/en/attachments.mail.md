Questions, confirmations and approvals belong to the record, not to one
person's mailbox. That is why emails can be attached as a file to any record
that has attachments — order, customer, supplier, lead, ticket and more.

## Storing an email

- Drag the email from Outlook straight onto the **Attachments** card — it is
  uploaded as `.msg`. A saved `.eml` file (for example from Thunderbird or
  Outlook on the web) works the same way, as does **Upload**.
- Drag one file onto the card at a time.
- Emails are only accepted on internal areas: attachments on records,
  documents and chat — not the customer portal, the helpdesk portal, the
  customer intake or the data request portal.
- The email is read in the background shortly after uploading; until then the
  view shows “The email is being read”.
- The attachment limits apply: size like any attachment, at most 100
  attachments in the email and embedded emails down to the second level. A
  damaged file, or a file that is only named like an email (such as a renamed
  Word document), is rejected with a message.

## Viewing an email

A stored email shows the **View email** icon. The view shows sender,
recipients, copy, date, subject and the text. If the email only has an HTML
part, workDiary shows the text derived from it.

- **Show original layout** shows the email as it was designed — isolated:
  without scripts, without forms and without images from the internet. Images
  contained in the email itself are shown.
- Embedded emails (forwarded "as attachment") can be expanded.
- If an email cannot be read, the view names the reason; the file stays
  unchanged and can be downloaded.

## Attachments of the email

- **Open** shows PDF files and images in the preview.
- **Download** saves any attachment; workDiary does not open it in the
  browser. File types outside the allowed list are delivered as `.bin` and
  marked in the list.
- **Keep as file** stores a copy of the attachment as a separate file on the
  same record. This requires the same permission as uploading; the same file
  types and sizes apply.

## Permissions and search

Anyone who may see the record can see a stored email. Subject, sender and
text go into the search (area **Attachments**): you find the email by a word
from the subject or the text. If an email is stored as a document in the
documents module, its text is also part of the document search.
