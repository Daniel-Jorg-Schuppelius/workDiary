Menu planning combines published recipes into menus and buffets. Each menu
has an event date and a guest count; dishes scale decimally by portions or
guests.

**Demand:** The menu aggregates the material demand of its dishes without
duplicated recipe items — the basis for purchasing and the manufacturing
order.

**Allergens:** Allergens are inherited from ingredients to dish and menu and
displayed; reasoned overrides are audited. Unresolved allergens block
release in the party-service context.

**Costs:** Planned costs per recipe and portion come from article costs;
the target/actual comparison runs via the manufacturing order and
post-costing.
