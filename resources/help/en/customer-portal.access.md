You sign in to the customer portal with your **email address** and your
**password**. With the **Stay signed in** option you do not have to log in
again on every visit on your own device. For security reasons the number
of login attempts is limited.

To protect your access you can set up **two-factor authentication**, which
requires a second proof in addition to your password. You can choose an
**authenticator app** (code generator), a **code by email**, or a
**security key or passkey**. Several methods can be active at the same
time. When you enable a method you receive one-time **recovery codes** –
keep them in a safe place, as they let you sign in if your second factor
is unavailable.

You manage the configured methods yourself and can remove them again. If
your organisation requires two-factor authentication, the last remaining
factor cannot be deleted. At your next sign-in the second factor is
requested in addition to your email and password.

**Profile and login email:** Under **Profile** you see your name, your login
email and the company your access belongs to. This is also where you change
the email address: a confirmation link is sent to the new address. Only after
you click it does it become your login email; the previous address receives a
notice. Until then you sign in with the previous address. The link is valid
for a limited time only; a new request replaces one that is still pending.
