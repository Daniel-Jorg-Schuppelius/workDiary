The payroll and social security area gathers the organization's
payroll-relevant master data: country, company number and responsible tax
office, plus the tax identifiers (tax number, VAT ID, register entry). The
latter are maintained together with the invoice/branding configuration.
Access requires the payroll management permission, held by HR and
management.

Its core is the minimum-wage overview. You maintain the history of hourly
rates, can load the statutory minimum-wage history for the organization's
country and import EU reference values from Eurostat. Based on the current
rate, employees with an hourly wage below the minimum wage are listed.

Affected hourly wages can be raised to the minimum wage for individual
people or for all those affected; every raise is recorded in a tamper-proof
audit log. The minijob threshold is shown as well. The actual payroll
handover to accounting happens via the payroll export.
