As accounting you own customer master data, billing and the handover to
the invoicing and payroll systems.

Typical flow:

1. **Maintain customers** (create, change, export) and check project
   billing settings.
2. **Approve time entries** or verify the approval state, **lock
   timesheets** (correction lock for the billing run).
3. **Billing handover**: transfer billable time and material to the
   leading invoicing program (e.g. DATEV/Lexoffice).
4. Create and deliver the **time export** for payroll (monthly approval
   required).
5. Manage **invoices** when no external invoicing program is leading.

What you can do:

- Fully manage **customers**; create, issue and mark **invoices** as
  paid.
- **Lock/unlock** and **export** timesheets.
- Manage **rates** and **time accounts**.
- Maintain **surcharge rules** (night/weekend/holiday surcharges).
- Read and export **reports**.

Important: after the handover, invoicing sovereignty lies with the
external program – transferred positions are locked against changes in
WorkDiary.
