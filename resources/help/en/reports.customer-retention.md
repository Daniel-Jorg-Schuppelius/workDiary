This report shows **how well the company retains its customers**.

- **Cohort retention**: customers are grouped by their **first service
  year** (org-wide, independent of the period filter). Each cell shows
  the share of the cohort that was still active in a given year.
- **Customer bridge**: base at period start → + new customers →
  + won back → − new-but-churned → − lost = base at period end.
  "Active" means: service within the configured threshold
  (default 365 days).
- **Returning rate**: share of last year's active customers who are
  also active in the reporting year.

The lists below the bridge name the affected customers — each entry
links to the customers & projects report.
