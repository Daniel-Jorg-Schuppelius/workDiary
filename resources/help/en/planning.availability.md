Maintain your own availability and shift wishes here. The planning takes
these entries into account when generating staffing suggestions.

Availability windows:

- Recurring by weekday or tied to a specific date.
- Kind: available, unavailable or preferred.
- Optional validity range for recurring windows.

Shift wishes refer to a specific date and optionally a shift type — as a
wish (I would like to take it) or an aversion (I would like to avoid it).

These entries are hints only: the final staffing is decided by the
planners. A window marked "unavailable" or an aversion removes you from
the suggestions.
