The knowledge base collects known problems and proven solution steps
from day-to-day work – linkable to orders and assets. You find it under
**Daily operations** → **Knowledge & docs** → **Knowledge**, tab
**Knowledge base**.

Typical workflow:

1. **Create article** (status “Draft”): **Title**, **Problem
   description**, **Solution steps**, optionally a **Collection**
   (further ones later via “Add to collection”), **Tags** and
   **Attachments** such as screenshots.
2. **Publish**: only then does the article appear in search and
   suggestions for everyone.
3. **Link**: on the detail page of an order or asset, the **Knowledge
   base** card shows the **Linked articles** and matching
   **Suggestions**; **Link** assigns an article, **Create article from
   this** creates a new one directly. In the article, the linked cases appear
   under **Problem history**.
4. Collect **feedback**: under “Was this article helpful?” every person
   can choose **It helped** or **It did not help** once; voting again
   changes your own vote. The list can be sorted by “Most helpful
   first”.
5. **Archive**: removes the article from search; it remains readable.

Important statuses: “Draft” → “Published” → “Archived”. The form has no
visibility field: published articles are visible to everyone with
access to the knowledge base, drafts and archived articles only to the
person who created them and to the editorial team.

Permissions: anyone with the **Create knowledge article** permission
may create and link articles. Creators edit their own drafts (permission
**Edit knowledge article**); publishing, archiving and editing all
articles is done by the editorial team with the **Publish/archive
knowledge article** permission. **Delete** (permission **Delete
knowledge article**) is reserved for administrators by default.

Next steps: link articles directly in the order – colleagues will find
the solution the next time a similar case occurs.
