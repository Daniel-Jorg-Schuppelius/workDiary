The knowledge base collects known problems and proven solution steps
from day-to-day work – linkable to work orders, assets, customers and
protocols.

Typical workflow:

1. **Create an article** (draft): title, problem description,
   solution steps, category (e.g. "Printer", "Network"), tags,
   optionally screenshots as attachments.
2. **Publish** (editorial permission): only then does the article
   appear in search and suggestions.
3. **Link** it to a work order, asset, customer or protocol – this
   builds the problem history.
4. Collect **feedback**: every person can vote "helpful" or "not
   helpful" once (your own vote can be changed).
5. **Archive**: removes the article from search; it remains readable.

Important statuses: "Draft" → "Published" → "Archived".
Visibility: "Internal (entire organization)" or "Team-based".

Permissions: appropriately authorized staff may create articles.
Drafts are edited by their creator; publishing and archiving are
handled by the editorial role. **Deleting is reserved for admins.**

Next steps: link articles directly from the work order – colleagues
will find the solution the next time a similar case occurs.
