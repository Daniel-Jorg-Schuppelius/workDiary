The facility structure arranges sites, buildings, floors and rooms in a
hierarchy. It provides location references for assets, assignments and
other operational data.

Create levels from outside to inside: site, building, floor, room. Use
clear names or numbers and maintain addresses and usage details where
required.

Before deleting a level, reassign its children and linked records. When
the organization changes, archiving is often better than removing a
location used by historical records.
