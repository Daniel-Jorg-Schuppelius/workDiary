With **Report a problem** you send technical issues to support directly
from the affected page — without hunting for log files or putting
screenshots together by email.

**How it works:**

1. Open the help sidebar and click *Report a problem* (also available on
   error pages).
2. Briefly describe what you expected and what happened instead.
   Optionally, you can attach screenshots.
3. Check the *Transmitted details* section: it shows exactly which
   technical information (page, app version, request ID) is sent along —
   **no order or customer data**.
4. Optionally include a *redacted diagnostic excerpt*. The preview shows
   the content before sending; sensitive values are removed
   automatically.

After sending you receive a **reference number** (e.g.
`PR-2026-0001`). Under *My problem reports* you can track the status.

The **request ID** on error pages helps support find the matching log
entries for your report — please mention it if support has any
questions.
