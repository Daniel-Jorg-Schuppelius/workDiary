**Report a problem** sends technical issues to support directly from the
affected page — no need to hunt for log files or email screenshots.

Open the help sidebar, click *Report a problem*, describe what you
expected and what happened instead. The *Transmitted details* section
shows exactly which technical information is sent — never order or
customer data. Optionally include a redacted diagnostic excerpt (with
preview). After submitting you receive a reference number and can track
the status under *My problem reports*.
