In the portal you can see the courses released to you and start them yourself.

**Overview:** Each course shows its title, scope and your progress. A
**preview** is available where the provider allows it, so you can see what a
course is about before starting.

**Booking and starting:** Courses that require enrolment are booked in the
portal; the learning units are then available in their intended order.
Completed units stay open as long as the course is released.

**Completion:** What counts as completion is defined by the course — for
example working through all units or passing an assessment. Completion is
documented and remains verifiable for you and your contact.

**If something is missing:** If you cannot see a course you expected, it has
not been released to you yet. Please contact your usual contact person.
