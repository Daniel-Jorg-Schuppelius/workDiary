On the **Training** page you find the courses your contractor has released
for customers. You preview courses, enrol or book them, work through the
learning units and complete the course.

## The course overview

Each course appears as a card with title and, where available, subtitle and
category. Depending on the course, the card also shows the **Price** and a
star rating: the average of the course feedback on a scale from 1 to 5,
visible only from five answers. If there are several categories, narrow the
list at the top under **Category**; **All categories** shows everything
again.

If you are already enrolled, the card shows your status and the **Open
course** button. Possible statuses are **Assigned**, **In progress**,
**Completed**, **Failed**, **Expired** and **Cancelled**.

If it shows **No training is currently released for you.**, there is
currently no course for you.

## Preview

If a course has preview units, **Preview** opens the **Course preview**
without enrolment: the course description and the texts of the preview units.
Media, exams and assignments are not part of the preview. For freely
accessible courses you can enrol there directly with **Enrol**; **Back**
returns you to the overview.

## Enrolling or booking

The course determines how you start it:

- **Enrol** – for freely accessible courses. You are enrolled immediately and
  the course opens.
- **Book course** – for bookable courses. The portal sends a booking request
  and confirms **Booking request sent.** Your contractor decides on it; once
  confirmed, you are enrolled and the card shows **Open course**. Clicking
  again does not create a second request. The price shown is fixed when the
  booking is confirmed.
- If it shows **Enrolment is arranged by your contact person.**, only your
  contractor can enrol you.

A course only appears in the overview within its availability period. An
existing enrolment remains accessible after that.

## Working through the course

**Open course** shows the learning units in their order with their texts. A
tick marks completed units. When you have worked through a unit, click **Mark
as done**.

Units can be locked:

- **Available from …** – the unit is only unlocked on this date.
- **After the previous unit** – the course has a fixed order; complete the
  previous unit first.

If a unit requires a minimum dwell time, it can only be completed after that
time; the portal then states the remaining minutes. If your access is limited
in time and has expired, you can no longer complete units.

## Completion

When all mandatory units are done, the course counts as completed. The course
page then shows **You have completed this course. The record is available to
the client.** and the status reads **Completed**. The completion is
documented and traceable for your contractor.

## Limits

- You only see courses that are released and explicitly intended for
  customers. If an expected course is missing, please contact your contact
  person.
- The course page in the portal shows the texts of the units; media and exams
  are not included here.
- You cannot unenrol or withdraw a booking request in the portal.
