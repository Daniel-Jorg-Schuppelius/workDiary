Use the sign-in form to log in to WorkDiary with your username and
password. Enter both fields and submit the form. After five failed
attempts, sign-in is locked for a short time to prevent abuse.

If two-factor authentication is active for your account, you are
redirected to a second step once username and password are correct.
There you confirm your identity with the second factor before sign-in
is completed.

The "Remember me" option keeps your session active beyond closing the
browser. Use it only on your own devices.

If you have forgotten your password, reset it using the email reset
link. You set up the second factor in your account under the security
settings.
