Under **Customize menu** you hide sections, subgroups and individual
entries of the side navigation you personally do not need — including groups
of the “New …” menu. The setting applies only to you and is stored on the
server (identical on all devices).

**Important:** Hiding is presentation only. Permissions, global search,
bookmarks and direct links remain unchanged.

## All functions

The **All functions** page is the safety valve: it lists every area with its
state —

- **visible** — linked directly;
- **hidden by me** — restorable with one click;
- **deactivated by the organization** — with permission a link leads to the
  “Feature scope” page;
- **not licensed** — with a short module description.

Nothing disappears beyond reach, no matter how lean your menu is.
