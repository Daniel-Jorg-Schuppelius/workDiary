Teams group employees into work or department units. A team has a name,
optional description and color, and a team lead. Members are selected from
the users of your own organization; the team lead is automatically kept as a
member and flagged within the team. Members can also be added or removed
individually afterwards.

The detail view shows the assigned members and the team's jobs. If a person
who is also the team lead is removed from the team, the lead is reset.

The workload view shows, for a chosen period, which tasks are assigned to
the team members across all jobs. Tasks overlapping the time window appear
per affected member; due dates are highlighted. You choose the period via
the from/to selector.
