Customers and suppliers are central master data for projects, invoices,
communication, travel and reports.

Maintain names, addresses, contacts, payment or billing data and
external identifiers. Before creating a record, check whether the
business partner already exists to avoid duplicates.

Changes to billing data affect future processes. Existing documents and
completed transfers keep their documented state. Prefer deactivation or
archiving over deleting historical relationships.
