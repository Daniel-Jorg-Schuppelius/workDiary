A rotation plan is a multi-week duty rhythm (e.g. two weeks alternating
early/late shift) that continues automatically. In the week grid you define
the shift type per week and weekday; days without an entry remain off.

Assignments anchor the rhythm per employee to an anchor week (Monday of
week 1); optional valid-from/until dates limit the period. The daily
roll-forward run creates draft shifts for the planning window — the
roll-forward can also be triggered manually at any time.

The pre-planning is deliberately subordinate: already planned shifts
(including manually changed ones) and approved absences are never
overwritten; the rotation plan only fills gaps.
