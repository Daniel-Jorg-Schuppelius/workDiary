The interface consists of the header at the top, the sidebar on the left and
the help on the right-hand edge. This article shows where to find each setting
and feature.

## Color scheme: light or dark {#farbschema}

You switch to dark mode in the header: click **Settings** (the icon with the
sliders) and then **Switch** next to **Color scheme**. Clicking again switches
back to light. Your choice is saved in your profile and applies on all devices.

The search is faster: press `Ctrl` + `K` (on a Mac `⌘` + `K`), type “dark” or
“dark mode” and choose **Toggle color scheme**.

In your profile (user menu → **Edit profile** → **Theme**) you can instead
choose a specific theme or **Automatic – follows system**: the appearance then
follows the setting of your operating system. The switch in the header replaces
a theme chosen in your profile with your organization's default light or dark
theme. Administrators define these colors under **Themes**.

## Language {#sprache}

You also change the interface language under **Settings** in the header, or in
your profile. German, English, French, Italian and Spanish are available.

## The header {#kopfzeile}

- **Search** (magnifying glass, `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `K`): finds pages, help topics and
  actions such as switching the color scheme, plus data like customers,
  projects and activities.
- **Time clock** and **running timer**: clock in, clock out and stop directly
  from the header if you use these features.
- **Chat**: appears if your organization uses the chat.
- **Bookmarks**: your saved pages; this is also where you bookmark the current
  page.
- **Notifications** and **Reminders**: the latest messages and due reminders
  with a counter.
- **Settings** (sliders): color scheme and language, and, depending on your
  permissions, switching the active organization.
- **User menu** (your name): among other things profile, working time model,
  calendar subscription, saved filters, two-factor authentication, API tokens,
  help center and sign out.
- **Help** (`F1`): the question mark in the header on small screens, the help
  rail on the right-hand edge on large screens.

## The sidebar {#seitenleiste}

- **Workspace** (top): limits the sidebar to the areas you need right now;
  **Show everything** removes the selection.
- **New …**: creates entries such as orders, customers or appointments. Outside
  input fields, the `n` key creates a new order directly.
- Below are the areas with their pages; the open page is highlighted.
- **Customize menu** (bottom): hides areas and entries for you only.
- **Collapse** (bottom): shrinks the sidebar to its icons. On small screens you
  open the sidebar with the menu icon on the left of the header.

## Can't find a feature? {#funktion-finden}

1. Press `Ctrl` + `K` and type a keyword. The search also knows everyday terms
   such as “dark mode”.
2. Search the help center. Many results offer **Go to page** and take you
   straight to the feature.
3. **All functions** (via **Customize menu**) lists every area with its status,
   including hidden ones and ones not enabled.

If a feature is missing there too, it is not part of your permissions or of
your organization's plan. Please contact your administrator.
