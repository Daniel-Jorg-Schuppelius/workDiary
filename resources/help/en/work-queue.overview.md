The work queue collects in one place what is currently waiting for you:
approvals (leave, expenses, month closings, overtime requests, time
corrections, daily close corrections, duty plans for approval, shift
exchanges, personnel file submissions, course bookings, supplier
self-assessments, admission requests to club groups, price changes, learning
time, approval chains), inboxes (assignment inbox including e-mail
clarifications, incoming invoices, invoice inquiries, customer intakes from
the portal, rental requests), tasks assigned to you (open issues, orders,
project tasks, service tickets) and reminders (your own drafts left open,
chat reminders).

**Project target times:** If people in a project are below their target time, one item per project shows the number of open findings; without the right to see all times you only see your own days. It is resolved on the project's time tab by acknowledging or booking late.

**Approvals in stages:** If your organisation requires two approvals for
overtime or time corrections, the request leaves your queue once you have
approved it — another person gives the second stage. Approved time
corrections then appear for those who may apply them.

**Shift exchange in two steps:** An exchange request addressed to you appears
as your personal item to accept. Only after acceptance does the exchange wait
for approval by planning; anyone involved in the exchange does not approve
it. Open hand-overs without a target person are offers and do not appear in
the queue.

**Customer intakes and club groups:** New customer intakes are shown to
everyone who may process them, accepted ones only to the assigned person.
Club administration sees admission requests for all groups, a group leader
for their own groups.

**Read here, process there:** The queue lists the items and links to the
page where you complete them. It has no status of its own; once an item is
done there, it disappears here.

**What you see:** Only what you would also see and be allowed to process on
the respective page — modules, plan and permissions apply unchanged. Your
own requests do not appear among your approvals. Shared inboxes appear
bundled with their count.

**Urgency:** Overdue, due soon (within three days) or open. The list is
sorted accordingly; filters narrow it to one area, one source or only items
assigned to you personally. The "Work queue" tile on the dashboard shows the
most urgent items.
