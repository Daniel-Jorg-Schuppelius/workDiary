The work queue collects in one place what is currently waiting for you:
approvals (leave, expenses, month closings, price changes, learning time,
approval chains), inboxes (assignment inbox including e-mail clarifications,
incoming invoices, invoice inquiries), tasks assigned to you (open issues,
orders, project tasks, service tickets) and reminders (your own drafts left
open, chat reminders).

**Read here, process there:** The queue lists the items and links to the
page where you complete them. It has no status of its own; once an item is
done there, it disappears here.

**What you see:** Only what you would also see and be allowed to process on
the respective page — modules, plan and permissions apply unchanged. Your
own requests do not appear among your approvals. Shared inboxes appear
bundled with their count.

**Urgency:** Overdue, due soon (within three days) or open. The list is
sorted accordingly; filters narrow it to one area, one source or only items
assigned to you personally. The "Work queue" tile on the dashboard shows the
most urgent items.
