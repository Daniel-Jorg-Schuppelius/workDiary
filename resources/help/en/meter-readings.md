Meter readings capture meter values, for example for electricity, water or
gas. Each reading belongs to an inventory item (asset) and records the read
value, the unit, the reading time and the person who took the reading.
Consumption is derived from consecutive readings.

In the overview you filter by asset and search by unit, asset or the person
who took the reading; sorting is by reading time, value, consumption or unit.
This makes consumption trends per meter traceable.

A new reading can be created directly from an asset's context. Implausible
values, such as a reading below the previous one, are rejected on entry.
Recording is available to people with the appropriate permission.
