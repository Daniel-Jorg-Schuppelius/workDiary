Under **Billing** you see your billing account month by month: how much time was worked for you, what it is worth, which payments have been received and which amount is outstanding. The menu item only exists if your contractor has released invoices to you and bills you through such a billing account.

## The month list

The **Billing** page lists the months, the newest at the top, 24 months per page. For each month you see:

- **Month** – a click opens the month view,
- **Hours** – the billable time of the month,
- **Total** – the value of this time,
- **Settled** – the payments assigned to this month,
- **Previous month** – the carry-over from the previous month,
- **Outstanding** – the balance at the end of the month: previous month plus total minus settled.

A month marked **provisional** has not been closed yet. Its values can change as long as your contractor adds times or payments; when your contractor closes the month, they are fixed. If an open month changes, the carry-over and the outstanding amount of the following months change as well.

## The month view

At the top you see the month, for an open month with **provisional**, and on the right the button **Download PDF**. The link **Billing** above takes you back to the month list.

The billing block shows four values:

- **Total** with the hours; if travel was charged, the travel time is shown separately next to it. The travel is included in the amount but does not count as working time.
- **Settled**,
- **Previous month**,
- **Outstanding**.

Below that, **Attendance** lists every billable assignment of the month:

- **Weekday** and **Date**,
- **Reason** – the type of activity,
- **Start** and **End**,
- **Duration**,
- **Travel** – only if travel time was charged in the month,
- **Amount** – the value of the assignment.

If there are payments in the month, they follow under **Recent payments** with date and amount. If a month has no assignments, it shows **No entries in this month.**

## Attendance record as PDF

**Download PDF** downloads the attendance record of the month with the attendance list and the billing block; an open month is marked **provisional** in it.

The link behind the button is valid for 24 hours and also works without signing in – so you can pass it on. If it has expired, open the month view again; a new link is created.

## Limits

- Billing is for viewing. Times, amounts and payments are entered by your contractor; you cannot change anything here.
- Payments only appear once your contractor has recorded them.
- You find the individual invoices under **Invoices**.
