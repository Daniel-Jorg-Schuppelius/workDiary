Under **Billing** you find your monthly overview: recorded attendance
(date, reason, start/end) and the billing block with total value, booked
payments, previous-month carry-over and outstanding balance.

Months that are not yet closed are marked **provisional** — values may
still change until the month is closed.

"Download PDF" provides the month's attendance record; the link is valid
for 24 hours and can be shared without logging in.
