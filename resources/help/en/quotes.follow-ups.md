The follow-up list shows sent quotes whose follow-up date has been reached —
kept separate from the document feed, which is bounded by a date range and
would let an overdue quote slip out of sight.

**Date:** On sending, the follow-up date is prefilled automatically (working
days after dispatch, configurable under *Settings → Quotes*). An existing
date is never overwritten. The date can be postponed at any time, for
example when the customer asks for more time.

**Following up:** Recording the contact creates a communication note in the
customer file — that is where it will be looked for later, not in a separate
quote history. The next date can be set right away.

**Reminder:** The nightly deadline scan reports due and overdue follow-ups
through the notification rules. Accepted and rejected quotes no longer
appear in the list.
