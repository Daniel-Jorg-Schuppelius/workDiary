“My club” is the member’s view: you see the upcoming events you may register for, your registrations, changes and your confirmed attendance. The page appears as soon as your user account is linked to a member or you are entered as a guardian of a member.

**Finding events:** Everything you may register for is shown — club-wide events, events of your groups (including courses, without a personal invitation, as long as the target group matches) and personal invitations. Registration and cancellation deadlines are shown at the event; if the event is full you land on the waitlist and move up automatically.

**Guardians:** Guardians explicitly choose the member they represent at the top. What a guardian may do (register, view attendance, receive messages) is set by the club administration per assignment. A revocation ends access immediately.

**My attendance:** Only what the leadership has confirmed counts; a registration is not a record. The total refers to the header period, the list shows every confirmed record. Absences of others, foreign member lists or contact data are not visible here.

**Messages:** Reminders, reschedules, cancellations and waitlist promotions reach you via your user account (according to the organisation’s notification rules), otherwise via the stored email address; guardians with the right “receive messages” likewise. Every message is delivered only once, delivery failures are visible to the administration at the event. A message does not count as read.

**My fees:** If you are set as the paying person of a fee account, you see your fee notices (also as PDF), due dates, booked payments, credit and open amounts. An exported collection counts as paid only once the credit is booked. Guardian rights for registrations are not enough — fee management sets the paying person explicitly.
