Tours combine stops and assignments in a planned order. The map view
supports route review and optimization.

First maintain the required customer or work-site addresses. Then
assign stops, responsibility and planned times, review the route and
start the tour only when the information is complete.

Automatic optimization is a planning suggestion. Check time windows,
priorities and real travel conditions before accepting it. Coordinate
changes to active tours with the people involved.
