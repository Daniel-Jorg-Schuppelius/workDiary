The grading module is optional. The club administration enables it in the club settings; without it, members, groups, events, attendance and fees work unchanged.

**Systems and grades:** Per discipline you create a system with named, ordered grades. Rank and name are the data, a colour is display only. There is no built-in belt order and no federation rule; every rule is club configuration.

**Rule versions:** Requirements apply per rule version. A new version takes over the rules of the active version as a draft; only activating supersedes the previous one. Existing exam offers keep their version.

**Requirements per target grade:** All entered items must be met: previous grade, minimum attendance in minutes or as a number of sessions, counting period (since previous grade, since joining or a fixed window), minimum waiting time in calendar months, minimum age on exam day, a mandatory course as confirmed proof and optionally the leader’s approval. Only confirmed attendance before the exam start counts; registrations and excused absences do not. Disciplines stay separate: events count only if their discipline or that of their target groups matches the system.

**Progress:** For each member the application shows the current grade and, for the next grade, what is met and what is missing, for example “18 hours 45 minutes of 20 hours; 1 hour 15 minutes remaining”. The counting period is defined by the rules; the header period does not change eligibility.

**Recognition and proofs:** A grade obtained elsewhere is recognised with date and evidence. Course proofs satisfy mandatory courses; external training records with origin and confirming person count only if the rule version allows it. A revocation is a separate, reasoned step and deletes nothing.

**Groups by grade:** Besides age, groups may require a minimum and maximum grade of a system. Without a valid grade there is no access. A new grade creates a change proposal where needed, which the leadership confirms; nobody is removed automatically.
