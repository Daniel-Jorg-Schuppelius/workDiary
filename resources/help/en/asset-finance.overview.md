The module manages leasing, hire-purchase, financing and usage contracts
as operational files — with terms, deadlines, usage limits and a
plan/actual view.

**Leasing file:** Each contract gets its own number (LEA-…), partner,
term, assets, cost centre and responsibilities. Terms (rate, special
payment, residual value, purchase option) are confidential financial
data with a separate permission.

**Activation** freezes the terms as a snapshot and generates the planned
rate schedule. **Deadlines** (termination, extension, purchase option,
return, final inspection) warn ahead of time and escalate.

**Actuals are references only:** incoming invoices are linked to rate
lines, meter readings feed usage limits — WorkDiary never posts anything.
Accounting (HGB/IFRS 16) and tax attribution stay with the accounting
system; the target audience is B2B (CCD II consumer rules are not checked).

**End process:** documents condition, mileage/hours, damages and the
decision — return, purchase, extension or replacement.
