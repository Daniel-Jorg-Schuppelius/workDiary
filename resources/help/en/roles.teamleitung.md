As team lead you run workforce planning, time approvals and the
operational order management of your area.

Typical flow:

1. Review and publish the **duty plan**, maintain shifts and coverage
   requirements.
2. **Approve time entries** and decide correction requests; record
   times **for others** when needed.
3. Create, assign and track **work orders** in the diary – also for
   other people.
4. Decide **absences**: approve or cancel vacation, maintain sick
   leaves.
5. **Lock/unlock timesheets** (prepare month-end closing) and read
   **reports** for your area.

What you can do:

- Create, edit and archive **projects**; maintain tasks and milestones.
- **Approve time** and **lock or unlock timesheets**.
- Manage **duty plans, shifts, on-call shifts**.
- View and manage the team's **time accounts (flex)**.
- Help shape **notification rules** (e.g. deadlines, escalations).

Not in your profile: invoices, customer master data maintenance,
platform administration.
